CBT Program

Module 5 • Mindfulness and Present-Moment Awareness

Overview
  • Introduce mindfulness as the practice of paying attention on purpose, in the present moment, without judgment.
  • Practice observing thoughts, emotions, and sensations with curiosity rather than criticism.
Key Takeaways
  • Mindfulness creates space between experience and reaction, supporting emotion regulation.
  • Short, consistent practices build awareness that supports other CBT skills.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Guided breath practice: Spend 5 minutes following your breath and noting when the mind wanders.
  • Mindful eating: Choose one snack or meal to experience with full sensory attention.
Homework
  • Commit to a daily 5–10 minute mindfulness practice using a guided audio or timer.
  • Record observations after each practice: mood shifts, sensations, and any judgments you noticed.
  • Experiment with placing a mindful pause before one routine activity (e.g., opening email, starting chores).
