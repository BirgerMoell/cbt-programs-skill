Module 4 • Move Your Body, Support Your Mood

Overview
  • Explore the mind-body connection, how movement influences brain chemistry, and why consistency matters more than intensity.
  • Experiment with brief physical activities that feel accessible right now.
Key Takeaways
  • Even gentle movement can boost endorphins, energy, and mood stability.
  • Planning activity into your day increases follow-through and confidence.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Movement menu: Identify 5 forms of physical activity ranging from low to moderate effort that feel realistic.
  • Body scan check-in: After a short walk or stretch, write down physical sensations and emotional shifts you notice.
Homework
  • Schedule at least three short physical activity sessions this week (e.g., 10-minute walk, light stretching).
  • Track how you feel physically and emotionally before and after each session.
  • Reflect on which activities felt most doable and why.
