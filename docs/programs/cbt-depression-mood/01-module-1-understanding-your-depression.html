CBT Program

Module 1 • Understanding Your Depression

Overview
  • Clarify what depression is, how it shows up across emotional, cognitive, physical, and behavioral domains, and why it deserves compassionate care.
  • Explore potential contributors—including biology, environment, and lived experiences—to build context for change.
Key Takeaways
  • Depression is a medical condition shaped by multiple factors; awareness replaces self-blame with informed care.
  • Recognizing early signs across body and mind helps you respond sooner.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Symptom mapping: Categorize current or past symptoms into emotional, cognitive, physical, and behavioral columns.
  • Origins reflection: Jot any life events, stressors, or patterns you believe influence your mood.
Homework
  • Capture two moments this week when you noticed depressive symptoms and describe what was happening.
  • List questions about depression you want to revisit with a clinician, coach, or support group.
  • Note any strengths or supports you already rely on when your mood dips.
