CBT Program

Body Wisdom: A Compassionate Approach to Weight & Body Image

Summary

A science-based, compassion-focused CBT program that helps you build a sustainable relationship with food, movement, and your body. Moving beyond diet culture, this program addresses the psychological roots of eating patterns, challenges body image distortions, and empowers you to make lasting changes rooted in self-care rather than self-control.

Condition: Weight Management & Body Image

Duration: 6 modules • 10-14 weeks self-paced
