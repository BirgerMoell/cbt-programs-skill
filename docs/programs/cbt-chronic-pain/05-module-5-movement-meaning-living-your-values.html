CBT Program

Module 5 • Movement & Meaning: Living Your Values

Overview
  • The final module integrates everything into a life of purpose. We move from 'managing pain' to 'engaging in life.'
  • We use 'Values Clarification.' Identifying what matters to you (family, nature, creativity) independent of your physical ability.
  • We set 'SMART Goals' that are values-based. e.g., 'I want to walk in the park (Goal) to connect with nature (Value).'
  • We create a 'Flare-Up Plan.' A toolkit for bad days so you don't panic or despair.
  • We discuss 'Communication.' How to talk to doctors, family, and employers about your needs without apologizing for your existence.
  • The concept of 'Neuroplasticity' is revisited as a source of long-term hope. Your nervous system can change.
  • Finally, we focus on 'Joy.' Actively scheduling pleasurable activities, because positive emotions are natural painkillers.
Key Takeaways
  • Pain takes up less space when your life is bigger.
  • Values give you a 'why' to endure the 'how' of pacing and practice.
  • A flare-up plan prevents a lapse from becoming a relapse.
  • You are the expert on your own body.

Making the container bigger

Imagine a cup of water with a spoonful of salt. It tastes salty. Now imagine a lake with a spoonful of salt. You can't even taste it.

The salt is your pain. We might not be able to remove the salt entirely. But we can add more water. We can make your life—the container—bigger.

When you fill your life with friends, hobbies, nature, and meaning, the pain is still there, but it is a smaller part of the picture. It doesn't dominate the flavor of your days.

Exercise 1

Values Compass: Identify your top 3 values. Brainstorm one small activity for each that you can do even on a bad pain day.

The Flare-Up Plan

You will have bad days. That is not a failure; it is reality. The difference is, now you will be ready.

Your Flare-Up Plan is your safety net. It includes: decreasing activity (but not stopping), using relaxation skills, talking kindly to yourself, and waiting it out with patience. You don't have to fix it; you just have to ride the wave.

Exercise 2

The Flare-Up Box: Physically create a box with comforts—heat packs, favorite photos, a list of coping statements, a funny book.

A life worth living

You have spent a lot of time fighting your body. Now it is time to partner with it. It is an imperfect vessel, yes. But it is the only one you have, and it can still carry you to beautiful places.

Walk gently. Breathe deeply. Live fully. You are ready.

Exercise 3
