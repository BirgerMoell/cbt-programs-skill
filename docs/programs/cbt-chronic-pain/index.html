CBT Program

Pain Management Mastery: Reclaiming Your Life

Summary

A compassionate, scientifically grounded CBT program for living well with chronic pain. Moving beyond the medical model, this program helps you decouple pain from suffering. You will learn to break the 'boom-bust' cycle, challenge fear-based thoughts, and retrain your nervous system to dial down the volume of pain while reclaiming a life of value.

Condition: Chronic Pain

Duration: 5 modules • 8-12 weeks self-paced
