Finding Silence Within: Habituation to Tinnitus

Summary

A specialized CBT program for tinnitus distress. While we cannot always silence the sound, we can silence the reaction. This program uses habituation, cognitive defusion, and attention training to move tinnitus from the foreground to the background of your awareness, restoring your peace of mind and quality of sleep.

Condition: Tinnitus

Duration: 6 modules • 8-10 weeks self-paced
