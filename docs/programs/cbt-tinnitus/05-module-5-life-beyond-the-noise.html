Module 5 • Life Beyond the Noise

Overview
  • Finalizing habituation. Moving from coping to living.
  • Exposure to 'Spikes.' Understanding that stress, salt, caffeine, or lack of sleep will make it louder temporarily, and that's okay.
  • Protecting hearing (earplugs) without over-protecting (phonophobia).
  • Setting goals that have nothing to do with hearing.
Key Takeaways
  • Habituation is fragile at first but becomes robust over time.
  • A spike is just a spike; it will settle.
  • You are reclaiming your life from the noise.

The unwanted roommate

Think of tinnitus as an annoying roommate. You didn't choose them. They talk too much. They are messy. But they are on the lease, and you can't evict them.

You have two choices: You can spend every day screaming at them to leave (misery), or you can learn to live your life while they hang out on the couch (freedom). Eventually, you stop noticing they are even there.
