Module 4 • Sleep Strategies for the Ringing Mind

Overview
  • Sleep is the biggest casualty of tinnitus. This module adapts CBT-I (Insomnia) for tinnitus.
  • Sound enrichment at night is non-negotiable. We create a 'Sound Pillow' or bedside setup.
  • We address the fear of quiet rooms.
  • Relaxation techniques (Body Scan) to lower physical arousal before bed.
  • Cognitive restructuring for 'I won't sleep' panic.
Key Takeaways
  • Silence is the enemy of tinnitus habituation at night.
  • You can sleep with sound; the brain is designed to filter out steady noise.
  • Relaxation lowers the perception of the sound.

The sanctuary of sound

The bedroom can feel like a torture chamber when it's dead quiet. The ringing screams. So, we banish the quiet.

Fill your room with the sound of rain, crickets, or white noise. This is not masking; it is 'sound therapy.' It gives your auditory brain something else to process, diluting the tinnitus signal. Wrap yourself in a blanket of safe sound.

Exercise 1

Sound Setup: Experiment with different sounds (Pink noise, Brown noise, Nature). Find one that is soothing, not annoying.
