Module 6 • Long-Term Maintenance & Continued Success

Overview
  • The final module focuses on maintaining your quit long-term and handling ongoing challenges.
  • We explore 'The Maintenance Phase'—understanding that quitting is a long-term process requiring ongoing attention.
  • You will learn 'Ongoing Relapse Prevention'—strategies for maintaining your quit as time passes and life changes.
  • The concept of 'Dealing with Slips' is addressed—how to handle lapses if they occur and get back on track.
  • We address 'Life Changes & Smoking'—how major life events (stress, celebrations, losses) can trigger smoking urges and how to manage them.
  • You will practice 'Problem-Solving for New Challenges'—addressing obstacles that arise as your smoke-free life evolves.
  • Finally, we write a 'Smoke-Free Commitment'—a personal statement of your commitment to staying quit long-term.
Key Takeaways
  • Maintaining a quit requires ongoing attention and strategies.
  • Life changes can trigger smoking urges; having plans helps.
  • Slips can happen; how you respond determines the outcome.
  • You have the tools and knowledge to stay quit long-term.

The maintenance phase

Quitting smoking isn't a one-time event—it's an ongoing process. As time passes, you'll face new challenges. Stressful events. Social situations. Life changes. These can trigger smoking urges.

But you have tools. You have strategies. You have experience. You know how to manage cravings. You know how to prevent relapse. You can maintain your quit.

The maintenance phase is about staying vigilant while also living your life. It's about having plans ready. It's about continuing to use your strategies.

Life happens

Life will bring challenges. Stressful jobs. Relationship problems. Losses. Celebrations. These events can trigger smoking urges, even months or years after quitting.

But you can prepare. You can anticipate. You can have plans. You can use your strategies. You can stay quit through life's ups and downs.

We're going to help you prepare for these challenges. We're going to build your resilience. We're going to ensure your quit lasts.

Your smoke-free future

You've quit smoking. That's an incredible achievement. But the work continues. Maintaining your quit is an ongoing commitment.

But it's also a gift. Every day you don't smoke is a victory. Every craving you ride out is strength. Every challenge you overcome is growth.

You're building a smoke-free life. One day at a time. One craving at a time. One challenge at a time. You can do this. You are doing this.

Homework
  • Review your Maintenance Plan monthly. Adjust as needed. Continue using your strategies. Stay vigilant.
  • Continue celebrating milestones. 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years—every milestone matters. Honor your progress.
  • If you experience a slip, use your Lapse Response Plan immediately. Don't let one cigarette become a relapse. Get back on track.
  • Share your Smoke-Free Commitment with someone you trust. Ask them to remind you of it if you ever struggle.
  • Continue building your smoke-free life. Every day is an opportunity. Every day you don't smoke is a success. You've got this.
