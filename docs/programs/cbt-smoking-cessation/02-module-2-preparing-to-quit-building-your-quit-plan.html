Module 2 • Preparing to Quit: Building Your Quit Plan

Overview
  • This module focuses on preparation—the critical phase before quitting that significantly increases success rates.
  • We explore 'Quit Date Selection'—choosing the right time to quit, considering stress levels, support availability, and life circumstances.
  • You will learn 'Trigger Identification & Planning'—anticipating your triggers and creating specific plans for managing them.
  • The concept of 'Alternative Coping Strategies' is introduced—building a toolkit of non-smoking ways to meet the functions smoking currently serves.
  • We address 'Social Support'—identifying people who can support your quit attempt and how to enlist their help.
  • You will create a 'Quit Plan'—a comprehensive, personalized plan for your quit attempt.
  • Finally, we practice 'Confidence Building'—addressing doubts and building belief in your ability to quit.
Key Takeaways
  • Preparation significantly increases quit success rates.
  • Having a plan for triggers prevents impulsive smoking.
  • Social support is crucial—quitting alone is harder.
  • Building alternatives before quitting makes the transition smoother.

The preparation phase

Many people try to quit impulsively. They throw away their cigarettes and hope willpower is enough. But willpower is finite. When cravings hit, when stress spikes, when routines are disrupted, willpower often fails.

Preparation changes this. When you prepare, you anticipate challenges. You build alternatives. You create support systems. You increase your chances of success.

The preparation phase is active. You're not just waiting to quit—you're building the foundation for success.

Your quit plan

A quit plan is your roadmap. It includes: your quit date, your triggers and how you'll handle them, your alternative coping strategies, your support system, your reasons for quitting.

This plan isn't rigid—it's flexible. You can adjust it. But having it gives you direction. When cravings hit, you know what to do. When challenges arise, you have strategies.

We're going to create your plan together. We're going to make it specific. We're going to make it yours.

Exercise 2

Build Your Alternative Toolkit: For each function smoking serves, list 3-5 alternatives. Include immediate strategies (breathing) and longer-term strategies (exercise, hobbies).

Building alternatives

Smoking serves functions. To quit successfully, you need alternatives. If smoking relieves stress, you need stress management techniques. If it's social, you need social alternatives. If it's habit, you need new habits.

We're going to build these alternatives now, before you quit. You'll practice them. You'll get comfortable with them. When you quit, they'll be ready.

This isn't about replacing smoking perfectly. It's about having options. Multiple tools for multiple situations. A toolkit for your smoke-free life.

Homework
  • Set Your Quit Date: Choose a date 1-2 weeks away. Consider: low-stress period, support available, time to prepare. Mark it on your calendar.
  • Practice Your Alternatives: Before your quit date, practice 3 alternative strategies daily. Get comfortable with them. Notice which work best.
  • Finalize Your Quit Plan: Write it down. Include quit date, triggers and plans, alternatives, support system, reasons to quit. Review it daily until quit date.
