CBT Program

Module 5 • Building a Smoke-Free Identity & Lifestyle

Overview
  • This module focuses on the longer-term work of building a smoke-free identity and lifestyle.
  • We explore 'Identity Change'—shifting from 'I'm a smoker' to 'I'm a non-smoker' or 'I'm an ex-smoker.'
  • You will learn 'Reward Systems'—celebrating milestones and rewarding yourself for staying quit.
  • The concept of 'New Routines' is introduced—building new habits and routines that don't involve smoking.
  • We address 'Social Changes'—navigating social situations, relationships with smokers, and building a smoke-free social life.
  • You will practice 'Values-Based Living'—identifying what matters to you and how quitting supports those values.
  • Finally, we create a 'Smoke-Free Lifestyle Plan'—a vision for your life as a non-smoker.
Key Takeaways
  • Identity change supports behavior change—seeing yourself as a non-smoker helps you stay quit.
  • Celebrating milestones reinforces your quit and builds confidence.
  • New routines replace old smoking routines, making quitting easier.
  • Living according to your values provides motivation to stay quit.

Who are you now?

When you quit smoking, you're not just changing a behavior—you're changing your identity. You're moving from 'I'm a smoker' to 'I'm a non-smoker.'

This identity shift matters. If you still see yourself as a smoker who's 'trying to quit,' every craving feels like a battle. But if you see yourself as a non-smoker, cravings are just temporary discomforts.

We're going to build this identity. Through actions. Through language. Through evidence. Every day you don't smoke is evidence: I am a non-smoker.

Exercise 1

The Identity Shift: Write 'I am a non-smoker' 10 times daily for one week. Notice how this affects your thoughts and cravings.

Building new routines

Smoking was woven into your routines. Morning coffee and cigarette. After meals. Work breaks. These routines are triggers. You need new routines.

We're going to build smoke-free routines. New ways to start your day. New ways to take breaks. New ways to handle transitions. Routines that don't involve smoking.

This takes time. But gradually, these new routines become automatic. They become your new normal. Smoking becomes a distant memory.

Exercise 2

Create Your Milestone Rewards: Plan rewards for milestones (1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year). Make them meaningful. Celebrate your progress.

Your smoke-free life

Quitting smoking isn't just about not smoking—it's about building a life where smoking doesn't fit. A life of better health. More energy. More money. More freedom.

What does your smoke-free life look like? What can you do now that you couldn't before? How does quitting support your values?

We're going to create a vision. We're going to build toward it. We're going to make your smoke-free life a reality.

Exercise 3

Design Your New Routines: Identify 3 old routines that involved smoking. Create new, smoke-free routines to replace them. Practice them for 2 weeks.
