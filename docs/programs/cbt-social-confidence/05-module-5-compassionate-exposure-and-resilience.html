CBT Program

Module 5 • Compassionate Exposure and Resilience

Overview
  • Integrate self-compassion practices to recover from awkward moments or setbacks.
  • Learn repair strategies when interactions feel off (naming, clarifying, humor, follow-up).
  • Strengthen supportive relationships and accountability for continued exposures.
Key Takeaways
  • Compassion turns stumbles into data instead of self-criticism.
  • Repair attempts often deepen connection and disprove catastrophic predictions.
  • Accountability partners help you keep practicing when avoidance urges return.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Write a compassionate letter to yourself after recalling a tough social moment, focusing on common humanity, kindness, and encouragement.
  • List three repair phrases you could use (e.g., "I noticed I got quiet—mind if we start over?").
  • Identify one support person and plan a brief weekly check-in to share exposure wins and challenges.
Homework
  • After your next exposure, complete a self-compassion break: Notice → Name feelings → Offer kindness.
  • Attempt one small repair or follow-up message if an interaction felt awkward; record the outcome.
  • Schedule or conduct your first accountability check-in.
