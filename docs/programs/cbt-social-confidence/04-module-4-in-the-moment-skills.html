CBT Program

Module 4 • In-the-Moment Skills

Overview
  • Practice body-based regulation to manage physical symptoms (breath, posture, progressive muscle relaxation).
  • Use attention-shifting techniques to focus outward rather than on internal self-monitoring.
  • Develop brief conversational tools (openers, curious questions, exits) to reduce performance pressure.
Key Takeaways
  • Regulating the body signals safety to the brain and steadies your presence.
  • Curiosity about others interrupts self-focused worry.
  • Prepared scripts ease the burden of improvising under stress.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Practice 4-6-7 breathing or box breathing twice daily for one week; log sensations before and after.
  • Run a "curiosity drill": pick three conversation prompts and ask a friend to role-play short exchanges.
  • Create a conversation card with three openers, three follow-up questions, and two polite exit phrases.
Homework
  • Use one body regulation tool during a real social interaction; jot the effect within an hour.
  • Practice redirecting attention outward by naming three details about the person or environment in conversation.
  • Carry and review your conversation card before attending a social event.
