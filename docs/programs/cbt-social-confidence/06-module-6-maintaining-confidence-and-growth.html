CBT Program

Module 6 • Maintaining Confidence and Growth

Overview
  • Review progress, update your fear ladder, and plan ongoing exposures.
  • Create early warning signs and coping plans for setbacks or avoidance creep.
  • Integrate identity statements that reflect your growing confidence.
Key Takeaways
  • Growth is maintained through regular practice, reflection, and recommitment.
  • Setbacks are data, not defeat—you can restart with the next small step.
  • Owning a confident identity comes from repeated evidence of courageous behavior.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • List top skills gained, favorite coping tools, and situations that now feel easier.
  • Update your fear ladder with new steps and note the next two exposures you will repeat or advance to.
  • Write three identity statements (e.g., "I am someone who reaches out first.") and pair them with evidence from recent weeks.
Homework
  • Schedule monthly review sessions to revisit your ladder, celebrate wins, and plan new challenges.
  • Create a relapse prevention plan: triggers, early signs, supports, and reset actions.
  • Share one insight or success story with a trusted person to reinforce your progress.

Progress Check
  • I attempted the exercises and homework.
  • I can summarize what I learned in 2-3 sentences.
  • I have a plan for when I will practice next.

You do not have to be perfect to move on. The goal is honest effort and reflection.
