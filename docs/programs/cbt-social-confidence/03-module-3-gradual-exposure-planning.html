CBT Program

Module 3 • Gradual Exposure Planning

Overview
  • Design a fear ladder that breaks intimidating social situations into doable steps.
  • Learn exposure guidelines: start manageable, stay long enough for anxiety to peak and ease, reflect afterward.
  • Plan support structures and rewards to reinforce courageous actions.
Key Takeaways
  • Exposure rewires fear by pairing feared situations with new, safe experiences.
  • Graded steps beat heroic leaps—you control the pace.
  • Celebrating effort keeps momentum alive.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Brainstorm 8–12 social situations and rate anticipated anxiety (0–100). Sort them into a ladder from easiest to hardest.
  • Choose two lowest-level items and script specific actions, locations, people involved, and coping supports.
  • Plan an after-action reflection template: What happened? How anxious (0–100)? What did you learn?
Homework
  • Complete at least one exposure from the lower rung. Stay until anxiety decreases by ~20 points or for an agreed time.
  • Complete the reflection template after each exposure, noting surprises and wins.
  • Reward yourself with a small celebration or soothing activity after exposures.

Progress Check
  • I attempted the exercises and homework.
  • I can summarize what I learned in 2-3 sentences.
  • I have a plan for when I will practice next.

You do not have to be perfect to move on. The goal is honest effort and reflection.
