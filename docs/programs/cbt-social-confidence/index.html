Stepping Into Connection: CBT Program for Social Confidence

Summary

A six-module CBT pathway for people who want to feel safer, steadier, and more connected in social situations. Learn how thoughts, body cues, and actions interact, practice brave experiments, and build self-trust through gradual exposure and compassionate reflection.

Condition: Social Anxiety Disorder

Duration: 6 modules • 4–5 weeks self-paced
