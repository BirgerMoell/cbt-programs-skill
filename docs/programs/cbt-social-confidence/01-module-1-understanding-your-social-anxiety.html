CBT Program

Module 1 • Understanding Your Social Anxiety

Overview
  • Map how thoughts, body sensations, and behaviors reinforce social anxiety and avoidance.
  • Clarify the values and relationships that motivate you to work on social confidence.
  • Build a compassionate, science-based understanding of why your nervous system reacts the way it does.
Key Takeaways
  • Avoidance is a learned safety strategy; when you change the pattern, your brain can update the threat level.
  • Naming triggers and values helps you choose intentional experiments.
  • Self-criticism is not a prerequisite for change; curiosity and kindness fuel persistence.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Create a Social Anxiety Cycle worksheet: Trigger → Thoughts → Body sensations → Behaviors → Short-term relief → Long-term cost.
  • Write your program intention: one paragraph on what social confidence would let you do more freely.
  • Identify three personal strengths or supports that can help during challenging moments.
Homework
  • Track two social situations this week. Capture the situation, automatic thoughts, body sensations, and what you did next.
  • Journal once about how avoidance protects you and where it also keeps you stuck.
  • Read your intention statement aloud daily to reinforce motivation.
