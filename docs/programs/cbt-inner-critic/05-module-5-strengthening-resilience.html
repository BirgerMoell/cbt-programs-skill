Module 5 • Strengthening Resilience

Overview
  • In this module, you will learn how to build resilience to better cope with adversities and setbacks, minimizing the influence of the inner critic.
  • We will explore resilience-building techniques that enhance emotional regulation and adaptive thinking.
  • A resilient mindset will help you manage challenges without resorting to self-criticism.
Key Takeaways
  • Understand the components of resilience.
  • Learn techniques to enhance emotional regulation.
  • Develop adaptive thinking patterns.
