CBT Program

Module 2 • Challenging Negative Self-Talk

Overview
  • In this module, you will learn how to challenge and reframe negative self-talk. We will explore cognitive distortions that fuel the inner critic and how to counteract them.
  • You will be introduced to evidence-based techniques to dispute and replace unhelpful thoughts with more balanced perspectives.
  • Practicing these techniques will help weaken the influence of your inner critic over time.
Key Takeaways
  • Identify cognitive distortions in your self-talk.
  • Learn techniques for disputing negative thoughts.
  • Develop healthier, more balanced perspectives.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Practice identifying and labeling cognitive distortions in your daily thoughts.
  • Use a thought record to challenge and reframe negative self-talk.
  • Develop counter-statements to replace critical thoughts.
Homework
  • Complete a thought record for at least one instance of negative self-talk each day.
  • Reflect on the impact of reframing thoughts in your journal.
  • Monitor changes in your mood and behavior as you challenge your inner critic.
