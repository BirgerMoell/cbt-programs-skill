CBT Program

Module 4 • Building Self-Esteem

Overview
  • This module is designed to help strengthen self-esteem, which can reduce the power of the inner critic. We will explore the relationship between self-esteem and self-criticism.
  • You will learn strategies to enhance self-worth and recognize your positive attributes, accomplishments, and capabilities.
  • By building self-esteem, you create a more resilient mindset that is less susceptible to critical self-talk.
Key Takeaways
  • Understand the link between self-esteem and the inner critic.
  • Learn strategies to build and maintain self-esteem.
  • Recognize and celebrate your strengths and achievements.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Create a list of personal strengths and achievements.
  • Develop a self-esteem action plan with specific, achievable goals.
  • Practice positive affirmations centered on self-worth.
Homework
  • Update your list of strengths and achievements regularly.
  • Track progress on your self-esteem goals and reflect on your experiences.
  • Incorporate daily positive affirmations into your routine.
