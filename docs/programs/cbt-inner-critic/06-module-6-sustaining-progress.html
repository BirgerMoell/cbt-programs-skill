CBT Program

Module 6 • Sustaining Progress

Overview
  • The final module focuses on maintaining the progress you've made and preventing relapse into old patterns of self-criticism.
  • We will review strategies for sustaining change and addressing setbacks effectively.
  • This module will empower you to continue your journey with confidence and self-compassion.
Key Takeaways
  • Recognize signs of relapse and how to address them.
  • Learn strategies for maintaining positive changes.
  • Develop a long-term plan for ongoing self-compassion and self-esteem.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Create a relapse prevention plan with specific strategies.
  • Identify support systems and resources for ongoing growth.
  • Reflect on your journey and celebrate your progress.
Homework
  • Review and update your relapse prevention plan regularly.
  • Engage with a support group or community to reinforce positive changes.
  • Set intentions for continued personal growth and self-improvement.
