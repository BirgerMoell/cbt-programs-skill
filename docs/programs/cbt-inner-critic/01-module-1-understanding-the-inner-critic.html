Module 1 • Understanding the Inner Critic

Overview
  • The first step in managing your inner critic is understanding its origins and impact. This module will explore how the inner critic develops and why it persists.
  • We will examine the common characteristics of an inner critic and how it affects your thoughts, emotions, and behaviors.
  • By identifying your specific inner critic patterns, you will gain clarity on the areas that need attention.
Key Takeaways
  • Recognize the origins of your inner critic.
  • Understand the impact of the inner critic on your daily life.
  • Identify specific patterns of critical self-talk.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Reflect on the origins of your inner critic by journaling about early experiences that may have contributed to its development.
  • List common phrases or thoughts associated with your inner critic.
  • Create a mind map of the impact of your inner critic on different areas of your life.
Homework
  • Complete a daily journal entry describing instances of critical self-talk.
  • Identify and note any triggers that exacerbate your inner critic.
  • Record your emotional and behavioral responses to your inner critic.
