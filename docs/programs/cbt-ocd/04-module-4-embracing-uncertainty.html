Module 4 • Embracing Uncertainty

Overview
  • The core of OCD is the intolerance of uncertainty. 'I need to know for sure.' This module targets the need for certainty.
  • We introduce thoughts like 'Maybe, Maybe Not.' When OCD says 'Did you lock the door?', we answer 'Maybe I didn't. I'm willing to risk it.'
  • We tackle 'Reassurance Seeking.' Asking others 'Is this okay?' transfers the anxiety to them and prevents you from learning to cope.
  • We practice 'Scripting for Uncertainty.' Writing stories where the bad thing *might* happen and accepting that possibility.
Key Takeaways
  • Certainty is an illusion; no one is 100% safe, OCD just pretends it can make you safe.
  • Agreeing with the obsession ('Yes, maybe I am a monster') disarms it.
  • Living with 'I don't know' is a skill you can build.

Maybe yes, maybe no

OCD asks: 'Are my hands clean?' You try to prove they are. OCD says: 'Are you sure?'

The way to win is to stop arguing. When OCD asks, 'Are my hands clean?', you say, 'Maybe not. Maybe they are covered in germs. I am going to eat this sandwich anyway.'

This shuts down the debate. You are refusing to play the game. You are choosing to live your life in the presence of uncertainty.
