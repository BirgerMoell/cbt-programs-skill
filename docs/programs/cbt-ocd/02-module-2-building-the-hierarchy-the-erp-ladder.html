Module 2 • Building the Hierarchy: The ERP Ladder

Overview
  • This module builds the roadmap for Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP). We create a 'Fear Hierarchy' or 'Ladder.'
  • You will list your triggers—situations, objects, or thoughts that cause distress—and rate them from 0-100 (SUDS: Subjective Units of Distress Scale).
  • We discuss 'Response Prevention.' This is the 'RP' in ERP. It means facing the trigger *without* doing the ritual. No checking, no washing, no asking for reassurance.
  • We identify 'covert' rituals. These are mental compulsions (praying, counting, neutralizing bad thoughts with good ones) that are harder to spot but just as damaging.
  • The concept of 'Habituation' is explained. If you stay in a cold pool, the water eventually feels warm. If you stay with anxiety without a ritual, the anxiety eventually fades.
  • We set rules for exposure: It must be prolonged, repeated, and without distraction.
Key Takeaways
  • We start small; we don't jump to the top of the ladder on day one.
  • Mental rituals (rumination) are just as reinforcing as physical rituals.
  • Anxiety always comes down eventually, even without a ritual. This is the 'Habituation Curve.'

Surfing the wave

When the urge to perform a compulsion hits, it feels like a tidal wave. You think, 'If I don't wash my hands, this feeling will last forever. I will explode.'

Here is the scientific truth: Anxiety is a wave. It has a peak. If you do nothing—absolutely nothing—the wave will crash and recede. It physically cannot stay high forever. Your body runs out of adrenaline.

ERP is learning to surf that wave. You stand on the board, feeling the terror, waiting for the drop. And when it drops on its own, your brain learns a powerful lesson: 'I am safe. I didn't need the ritual.'
