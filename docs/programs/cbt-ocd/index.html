Breaking the Ritual: Reclaiming Your Agency

Summary

A specialized, gold-standard program based on Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP). This program helps you distinguish between 'ego-dystonic' intrusive thoughts and reality. You will learn to face your fears without performing rituals, tolerate the distress of uncertainty, and retrain your brain to stop sending false 'danger' signals.

Condition: Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

Duration: 7 modules • 10-14 weeks self-paced
