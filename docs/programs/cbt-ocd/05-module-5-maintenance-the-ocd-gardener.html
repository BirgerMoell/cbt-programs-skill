Module 5 • Maintenance: The OCD Gardener

Overview
  • OCD is chronic but manageable. We shift to a maintenance mindset.
  • We identify 'Lapse vs. Relapse.' A lapse is doing a ritual once; a relapse is falling back into the cycle.
  • We create a 'Willingness Plan.' Being willing to have the thoughts return without engaging them.
  • We discuss lifestyle factors: Stress, sleep, and alcohol can lower the threshold for obsessions.
  • Celebration of reclaiming values. Doing the things you love that OCD stole from you.
Key Takeaways
  • Recovery is not the absence of intrusive thoughts; it is the absence of rituals.
  • You can have a noisy brain and a quiet life.
  • Vigilance against 'sneaky' new rituals is key.

The garden of the mind

Think of your mind as a garden. OCD is a weed. We have pulled up the big weeds. Now, we must watch for the little sprouts.

You don't need to be afraid of the weeds. You have the tools to pull them. You are the gardener. You decide what grows here.
