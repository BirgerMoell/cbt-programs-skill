CBT Program

Module 3 • Facing the Fear: Exposure Begins

Overview
  • We begin active exposure. You will engage with the first item on your ladder.
  • We guide you through the process: Trigger the anxiety, resist the compulsion, and wait for the SUDS score to drop by half.
  • We address 'Inhibitory Learning.' The goal isn't just to lower anxiety, but to violate your expectations. 'I thought X would happen, but Y happened.'
  • Troubleshooting: What if the anxiety doesn't drop? (Answer: We wait longer, or we accept the discomfort as the new normal).
  • We discuss the 'Backdoor Spike.' Sometimes anxiety gets worse right before it gets better. This is called an 'Extinction Burst.'
Key Takeaways
  • The only way out is through.
  • You are retraining your amygdala (fear center) through direct experience.
  • Discomfort is not danger.

Leaning into the spike

This week, we touch the doorknob. We leave the shoes askew. We think the 'bad' thought on purpose.

It will feel wrong. It will feel irresponsible. Your brain will scream, 'Don't do this!' But remember: OCD is a liar. By defying it, you are reclaiming your territory.

Hold your ground. Watch the clock. Notice that you are still breathing. Notice that the world has not ended.

Homework
