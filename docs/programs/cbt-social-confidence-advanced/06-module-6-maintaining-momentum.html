CBT Program

Module 6 • Maintaining Momentum

Overview
  • ### Introduction: Lapse vs. Relapse You have built new skills, but old pathways in the brain remain. A bad day (a **Lapse**) is inevitable. It does not mean you are back at square one (**Relapse**). We will create a plan for when the old anxiety flares up, viewing it as a signal to practice, not a sign of failure.
  • ### Section 2: Expanding the Circle Now that you have tools, it is time to look at the "Opportunity Cost" of your anxiety. What have you been putting off? A career change? Dating? A class? We will update your Fear Ladder to include these life-enhancing goals. We move from "surviving" social situations to "designing" your social life.
  • ### Section 3: Identity Shift Ultimately, we want to shift your identity. You are no longer "A Socially Anxious Person." You are "A person who values connection and is willing to feel fear to get it." This subtle shift empowers you to act in alignment with your values, regardless of the butterflies in your stomach.
Key Takeaways
  • Confidence is a result of accumulated evidence; keep generating evidence.
  • A bad interaction is just data. It helps you refine your experiments.
  • You don't need to be 'cured' of all anxiety to live a full, rich life.

Exercises
  • **The Future Blueprint** Identify 3 upcoming events in the next 6 months that usually trigger you. Write a "Coping Plan" for each using the tools from Modules 1-5.
  • **Identity Statements** Write 3 new "I am" statements based on your work (e.g., "I am someone who speaks up even when my voice shakes").
  • **The 30-Day Challenge** Set one "Stretch Goal" to complete within the next month—something that excites and scares you.
Homework
  • Schedule a monthly 'Self-Check-In' to review your Fear Ladder.
  • Teach a concept from this course to someone else (or write it in a journal) to solidify your mastery.
  • Celebrate! Acknowledge the hard work you have done to step into connection.
