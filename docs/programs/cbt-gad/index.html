Unwinding the Worry: A Path to Freedom from Generalized Anxiety

Summary

A comprehensive seven-module CBT journey designed to help you break free from the cycle of chronic worry and catastrophic thinking. Learn to tolerate uncertainty, distinguish between helpful planning and harmful rumination, implement 'worry time,' and retrain your brain to stay present rather than fearing the future.

Condition: Generalized Anxiety Disorder

Duration: 7 modules • 8-12 weeks self-paced
