CBT Program

Module 7 • Maintaining Momentum

Overview
  • Consolidate progress and design a relapse‑prevention plan you’ll actually use.
  • Commit to core routines, schedule reviews, and keep learning with self‑compassion.
Key Takeaways
  • Review, refine, recommit—this is how habits stick.
  • Support beats solo: share one insight with someone you trust.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Looking back: capture top 2–3 skills, 1–3 changes made, and strengths you used.
  • Relapse plan: list 2–3 triggers, 2–3 early warning signs, and 3–5 "get back on track" actions.
  • Schedule your first 15–30 min monthly "Personal System Review."
Homework
  • Write a one‑sentence commitment to 1–3 core routines (e.g., daily plan, weekly review).
  • Put the first monthly review on your calendar; add review prompts that matter to you.
  • Share one insight with a support person; note their suggestion.
  • Create your quick‑reference list: "My Go‑To ADHD Strategies" (5–7 items).

Progress Check
  • I attempted the exercises and homework.
  • I can summarize what I learned in 2-3 sentences.
  • I have a plan for when I will practice next.

You do not have to be perfect to move on. The goal is honest effort and reflection.
