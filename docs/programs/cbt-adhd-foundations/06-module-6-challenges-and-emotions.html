CBT Program

Module 6 • Challenges and Emotions

Overview
  • Use a 5‑step problem‑solving model to move from overwhelm to action.
  • Build emotional regulation basics: notice & name, STOPP, grounding, and self‑soothing.
Key Takeaways
  • Pause creates possibility: a breath can change the next decision.
  • You can’t always choose feelings—but you can choose your next step.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Pick one recurring problem. Write Step 1–5: define → brainstorm → evaluate → implement → review (brief bullets).
  • Practice STOPP on a recent surge: Stop, Breath, Observe, Pull Back, Proceed. Write one sentence on what would change.
  • Try one grounding technique today (Box Breathing or 5‑4‑3‑2‑1).
Homework
  • Work through all 5 problem‑solving steps on paper; schedule and execute the first action.
  • Track 2–3 emotional triggers across the week; log which technique you tried and its effect.
  • Maintain your "homes" and paper flow; declutter one new tiny area once.
  • End‑of‑week reflection: what you learned about triggers, tools, and what helps fastest.
