CBT Program

Module 4 • Enhancing Focus

Overview
  • Attention is a trainable muscle—create the right conditions and practice gentle redirection.
  • Reduce external distractions (space, noise, interruptions) and manage internal ones (thoughts, worries, restlessness).
  • Use a Distraction Pad and 3‑minute breath practice to notice and return without self‑blame.
Key Takeaways
  • Environmental tweaks compound.
  • Noticing and returning is the practice. Judgment is optional.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Make a distraction inventory: top 2 external and top 2 internal distractors.
  • Implement one environmental change today (e.g., clear desk zone, earplugs, "focus spot").
  • 3‑minute breath focus: notice wandering, gently return; jot one sentence about the experience.
Homework
  • Use a Distraction Pad during two focus sessions; review afterwards.
  • Daily: one short attention practice (breath, grounding, or intentional movement). Track which felt most helpful.
  • Adopt at least one environmental change for the full week; note effects on focus.
