Module 2 • Building Structure and Routine

Overview
  • Structure reduces decision fatigue and creates gentle predictability—both are ADHD super‑supports.
  • Start with foundational anchors (sleep, wake, meals) and tiny morning/evening routines. Keep them short and winnable.
  • Improve time awareness with estimation drills and simple paper‑based time blocking.
Key Takeaways
  • Anchors turn good intentions into low‑friction defaults.
  • Time awareness is a trainable skill; practice beats guessing.

No narrative sections.

Exercises
  • Design a 3‑step morning and 3‑step evening routine you can actually do this week.
  • Time estimation drill: estimate one task, time it, compare. Repeat daily.
  • Create a simple paper time‑block for tomorrow (fixed items → anchors → 2–3 flexible blocks).
Homework
  • Track target vs. actual wake/sleep for 7 days.
  • Each evening, map tomorrow with simple time blocks and name one Must‑Do, one Should‑Do, one Want‑To‑Do.
  • End‑of‑week reflection: what stuck, what slipped, what surprised you about time?
