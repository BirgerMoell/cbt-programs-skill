Module 5 • Compound Profiles: Knowing the Terrain

Overview
  • Not all psychedelics are the same. A psilocybin mushroom experience is vastly different from an MDMA therapy session.
  • This module outlines the duration, specific effects, and unique safety considerations for the major compounds.
  • Harm reduction requires knowing specifically what you are taking and how it behaves.
Key Takeaways
  • Psilocybin (Mushrooms): Earthy, emotional, 4-6 hours. Wavy intensity.
  • LSD (Acid): Electric, analytical, 8-12 hours. Long duration requires stamina.
  • MDMA (Ecstasy/Molly): Empathogen, heart-opening, reduces fear. 4-6 hours. High risk of dehydration/overheating.
  • Ketamine: Dissociative, separates mind from body. 45-90 mins. Used for depression.

The Tryptamines: Psilocybin and LSD

Psilocybin is often described as having an 'organic' or 'entity' feel. It comes in waves. It is very internal. Dose matters immensely (1g is recreational, 5g is heroic/shamanic).

LSD is more 'digital' and stimulating. Because it lasts 12 hours, you must clear the entire day and the next. Sleep will be difficult afterward.

Safety note: Reagent testing is mandatory. Many synthetic imposters (like NBOMe) are sold as LSD and can be fatal.

The Empathogen: MDMA

MDMA is not a classic psychedelic; it dampens the fear center (amygdala) rather than dissolving the ego. This makes it excellent for trauma work.

It depletes serotonin. The 'come down' 2 days later can be rough (the 'Tuesday Blues'). Supplements (pre and post) are recommended.

Physical safety: MDMA raises body temperature. Sipping water is vital, but drinking too much water without electrolytes is dangerous (hyponatremia).

The Dissociative: Ketamine

Ketamine acts on Glutamate, not Serotonin. It creates a 'floaty' detachment.

At high doses ('K-Hole'), you lose awareness of the body. You must be in a safe, lying-down position to prevent falls.

It is currently the only federally legal psychedelic-style therapy in many places.
