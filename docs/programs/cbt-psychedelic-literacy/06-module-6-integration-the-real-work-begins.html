Module 6 • Integration: The Real Work Begins

Overview
  • The psychedelic experience is just the class; integration is the homework. Without integration, the experience is just a fleeting memory.
  • This module covers the 'Neuroplastic Window'—the critical 2–4 weeks post-experience.
  • Techniques for grounding insights into daily behavior.
Key Takeaways
  • Integration is the process of weaving the insights from the 'altered' state into the 'default' state.
  • The 'Afterglow' is a biological period of increased flexibility. Use it to build new habits.
  • Avoid making major life changes (quitting jobs, divorce) for at least 2 weeks after a deep journey.
  • Community and sharing help solidify the memory.

The Neuroplastic Window

After the substance leaves your body, your brain remains malleable. This is the time to start meditation, change your diet, or start therapy.

If you return immediately to a high-stress environment, the 'snow grooves' of the DMN will re-form exactly as they were.

Protect your peace. Keep the schedule light for the days following.

Exercise 1

Integration Journaling: Write a letter to yourself from the perspective of your 'higher self' or the insight you hope to gain.

Capture and Embody

You will forget the feeling. You must capture it. Journaling, voice memos, and art are essential immediately after.

Embodiment: If you learned 'I need to love myself,' what does that look like on a Tuesday morning? Does it mean sleeping more? Eating better? Setting boundaries?

Integration is often boring. It's the slow, steady work of change.

Exercise 2

Habit formation: Identify one tiny, physical action you can do daily to anchor a spiritual insight.

Dealing with ontological shock

Sometimes experiences are so profound they shake your worldview (Ontological Shock). You may feel alienated from 'normal' society.

Grounding is key: Eat root vegetables, walk barefoot in nature, exercise. Connect with the body to bring the mind back to earth.

Seek an 'Integration Circle' or therapist who understands psychedelic integration. You need a place to speak your truth without judgment.

Exercises
  • Integration Journaling: Write a letter to yourself from the perspective of your 'higher self' or the insight you hope to gain.
  • Habit formation: Identify one tiny, physical action you can do daily to anchor a spiritual insight.
Homework
  • Find a community: Look up a local or online psychedelic integration circle.
  • The 'No' List: Create a list of 3 things you will NOT do for 7 days post-experience (e.g., no social media, no alcohol, no arguing).
