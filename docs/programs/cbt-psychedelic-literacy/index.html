Psychedelic Literacy: Science, Safety, and Navigation

Summary

A comprehensive, science-based program designed to demystify psychedelic experiences. Synthesizing ancient wisdom with modern neuroscience, this course covers the mechanisms of action in the brain, the critical importance of set and setting, navigation techniques for altered states, and the art of integration to maximize safety and therapeutic potential.
