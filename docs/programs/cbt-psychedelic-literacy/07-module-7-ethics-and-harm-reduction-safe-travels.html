Key Takeaways
  • Harm reduction puts safety above judgment.
  • Spiritual Bypassing: Using psychedelics to escape human problems rather than face them.
  • Consent is paramount. You are in a vulnerable state; ensure your sitters/guides are vetted.
  • Know your laws. These substances remain illegal in many jurisdictions. Legal safety is part of set and setting.

Medical Safety

Psychedelics are physically non-toxic generally, but dangerous for specific hearts and minds.

Heart conditions: Stimulants (MDMA/LSD) raise blood pressure. Consult a doctor.

Mental Health: Personal or family history of Schizophrenia or Bipolar 1 is the primary contraindication. Psychedelics can trigger latent psychosis.

Medication interactions: Lithium and Tramadol can be fatal with psychedelics. MAOIs require strict diet changes.

The Zendo Principles

The Zendo Project provides psychedelic peer support. Their 4 pillars are: 1. Safe Space, 2. Sitting, not Guiding, 3. Talk through, not down, 4. Difficult is not bad.

If you are sitting for a friend, do not try to psychoanalyze them. Just be a calm presence. Offer a blanket and water.

Avoiding the 'Messiah Complex'

A common trap is feeling like you have 'figured out the universe' and need to convert everyone. This is ego inflation.

Chop wood, carry water. True enlightenment makes you more humble, not more arrogant.

Respect that this path is not for everyone.
