Module 4 • Navigation: Surrender and The Difficult Experience

Overview
  • Once the experience begins, how do you steer? The paradox is that you steer by letting go of the wheel.
  • This module teaches the 'Flight Instructions': Trust, Let Go, Be Open.
  • We cover breathwork as an anchor and how to transform a 'bad trip' into a 'challenging but educational experience'.
Key Takeaways
  • The primary resistance is fear of ego dissolution. The solution is surrender.
  • Technique: 'In and Through.' If you see a monster, ask it what it has to teach you. Do not run away.
  • Breath is the remote control: Long, slow exhales activate the parasympathetic nervous system.
  • Music can be changed to shift the emotional tone if you get stuck.

The art of surrender

The most common cause of anxiety is fighting the experience. You cannot stop the roller coaster once it starts. The safest posture is total relaxation.

Mantra: 'Trust, Let Go, Be Open.' Trust the compound and your brain. Let go of control. Be open to whatever arises, even if it is weird or scary.

Visualizing: Imagine floating on your back in a river. If you thrash, you drown. If you float, the current supports you.

Exercise 1

Breathwork practice: Practice 'Box Breathing' (4-4-4-4) for 5 minutes. This is your emergency brake.

Navigating the 'Bad Trip'

We prefer the term 'Challenging Experience.' These are often where the most work is done. They usually point to repressed emotions.

If you feel terror: Do not curl into a ball. Sit up. Breathe. Connect with your sitter. Change the music.

The confrontation technique: If a scary image appears, move toward it. Ask: 'What are you? What do you have to show me?' usually, the monster transforms into a lesson.

Exercise 2

Visualization: Close your eyes and imagine a door you are afraid to open. Visualize yourself opening it and hugging whatever is behind it.

Somatic anchoring

You have a body. Use it. If your mind spins, touch the floor. Feel the texture of a blanket. Drink water.

Singing or humming stimulates the Vagus nerve and can instantly shift a mood from fear to power.
