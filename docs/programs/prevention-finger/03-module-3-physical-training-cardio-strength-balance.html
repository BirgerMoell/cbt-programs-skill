Module 3 • Physical Training: Cardio, Strength, & Balance

Overview
  • The FINGER physical protocol was rigorous: it included cardio, strength, and balance. We break this down.
  • Cardio (Aerobic) training improves blood circulation to the brain, delivering oxygen and nutrients.
  • Strength training (Muscle) releases myokines that support brain health.
  • Balance training challenges the cerebellum and proprioception.
  • We create a graduated plan, starting where you are and building up.
Key Takeaways
  • Exercise is arguably the strongest medicine for the brain.
  • Psychomotor speed improved by 150% in the intervention group—largely due to movement.
  • You need a mix of heart-pumping and muscle-building activity.

Pumping oxygen upstairs

When you walk briskly or lift a weight, your heart pumps harder. This pushes blood into the micro-vessels of the brain. It is like watering a wilting plant.

The study showed that physical activity didn't just help the body; it helped the mind process information faster. Movement is cognitive maintenance.
