Module 6 • Social Activity: The Human Network

Overview
  • The FINGER study included group sessions. Social isolation is a major risk factor for dementia.
  • Socializing is a complex cognitive task. It requires listening, processing, reading faces, and recalling names.
  • Building a 'Social Portfolio.' diversifying your interactions.
  • Combining social + physical (e.g., walking groups) for a double benefit.
Key Takeaways
  • Loneliness is toxic to the brain.
  • Social interaction drives complex memory improvement (40% higher in the intervention group).
  • You need 'active' socializing, not just being in a room with people.

The ultimate brain game

We think of sudoku as a brain game, but a conversation is much harder. You have to remember history, read emotional cues, think of a response, and filter impulses—all in milliseconds.

Socializing is the most complex thing we do. The study showed that the group dynamics were crucial. We heal and grow in community.
