Key Takeaways
  • High blood pressure is a silent killer of cognitive function.
  • Managing metabolic factors (sugar, lipids) reduces the risk of inflammation.
  • You are the project manager of your cardiovascular numbers.

The plumbing matter

Imagine a high-performance computer. It needs a cooling system and a power supply. Your vascular system provides both. If the pipes (arteries) are clogged or under too much pressure, the computer overheats and slows down.

By keeping your blood pressure and cholesterol in check, you are protecting the delicate wiring of your mind.
