The FINGER Protocol: Mastering Brain Health

Summary

Based on the groundbreaking FINGER study—the first trial in the world to prove that lifestyle changes can prevent cognitive decline. This comprehensive, multidomain program guides you through the five pillars of brain health: nutrition, physical activity, cognitive training, social engagement, and vascular monitoring. It is a roadmap to building a resilient, sharper mind at any age.
