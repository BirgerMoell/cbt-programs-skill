Module 2 • The Nordic Brain Diet

Overview
  • Nutrition is fuel for the brain. We adopt the 'Nordic Nutrition Recommendations' used in the study.
  • Focus on anti-inflammatory foods: Berries, fish (Omega-3s), whole grains, and rapeseed oil.
  • We address the connection between gut health and brain health.
  • Reducing sugar and saturated fats to lower the risk of oxidative stress.
  • Practical meal planning: shifting from 'restriction' to 'nourishment.'
Key Takeaways
  • The Nordic Diet is similar to the Mediterranean diet but adapted for different staples.
  • Food directly influences inflammation, which affects cognitive speed.
  • Consistency in diet is more important than perfection.

Feeding the neurons

Your brain is an expensive organ. It consumes 20% of your energy. If you feed it low-quality fuel, it will sputter. In the FINGER study, participants were supported in eating a balanced diet rich in nutrients.

We aren't just eating for a waistline; we are eating for white matter. Every time you choose fish over processed meat, or berries over candy, you are fighting oxidative stress in your brain.
