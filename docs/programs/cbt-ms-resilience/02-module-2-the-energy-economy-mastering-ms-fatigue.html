Module 2 • The Energy Economy: Mastering MS Fatigue

Overview
  • Fatigue is the most debilitating symptom of MS. It is not sleepiness; it is a power failure. This module teaches you to become an elite economist of your own energy.
  • We introduce 'The Spoon Theory' and CBT Pacing. You will learn to stop the 'Boom and Bust' cycle where you do too much on good days and crash for three days after.
  • Rest is not laziness. In the world of MS, rest is a weapon. It is a strategic recharge.
Key Takeaways
  • The 'Boom and Bust' cycle destroys your stamina; consistency builds it.
  • You have a limited energy budget every day. Spend it like gold.
  • Strategic Rest (pre-emptive resting) prevents the crash.

The Boom and Bust Trap

You wake up feeling good, so you clean the house, go to work, and walk the dog. By 4 PM, you hit a wall so hard you can't speak. You spend the next two days in bed. This is the 'Boom and Bust' cycle.

It feels productive, but it is destructive. It keeps you in a state of crisis. We are going to replace this with 'Pacing'—the art of doing a little less today so you can do a little more tomorrow.

The Spoon Theory

Imagine you start the day with 12 spoons. A shower costs 1 spoon. Work costs 6. Stress costs 3. If you use 13, you borrow from tomorrow. And tomorrow charges high interest.

We will use Activity Scheduling to budget your spoons so you never go into energy debt.

Exercises
  • Energy Audit: Track your activities for one day. Assign a 'Spoon Cost' to each. Did you go over budget?
  • The 45/15 Rule: Try working/doing chores for 45 minutes, then forcing a 15-minute rest (no phone, eyes closed) *before* you feel tired.
Homework
  • The 'No' Challenge: Say 'no' to one non-essential demand this week to save a spoon for something you actually love.
