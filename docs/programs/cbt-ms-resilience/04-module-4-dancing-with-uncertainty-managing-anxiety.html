Module 4 • Dancing with Uncertainty: Managing Anxiety

Overview
  • MS is the disease of 'What If?' What if I relapse? What if I need a wheelchair? This uncertainty is a heavy psychological torment.
  • This module teaches you to tolerate uncertainty. We use mindfulness and grounding to pull you out of the terrifying future and back into the manageable present.
  • You will learn to distinguish between 'Prudent Preparation' and 'Catastrophic Worrying.'
Key Takeaways
  • Worrying about a relapse does not prevent it; it only ruins today.
  • Hyper-vigilance (scanning your body for symptoms) amplifies pain.
  • Control what you can (diet, sleep, stress); surrender the rest.

The Sword of Damocles

Living with MS feels like sitting under a sword that could drop at any moment. You spend your life looking up at the sword instead of looking around at your life.

We cannot remove the sword. But we can teach you to stop staring at it. We call this 'Uncertainty Tolerance.' It is the superpower of the chronically ill.

Body Scanning vs. Body Awareness

There is a difference between noticing a symptom and obsessing over it. 'My foot is tingling' is data. 'My foot is tingling, which means I'm relapsing, which means I'll lose my job' is a spiral.

We will use 'Grounding Techniques' to stop the spiral before it crashes.

Exercises
  • The 'What If' vs. 'Then I Will' Game: For every scary 'What If' thought, write a practical 'Then I Will' plan. (e.g., 'What if I can't drive?' -> 'Then I will use ride-share and save on insurance').
  • 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding: When fear hits, name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
Homework
  • Worry Time: Set a timer for 15 minutes a day. You are allowed to worry as hard as you want. When the timer goes off, worry time is over. Go live.
