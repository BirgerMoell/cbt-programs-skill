Module 2 • The Spotlight Effect & Cognitive Distortions

Overview
  • We tackle the 'Spotlight Effect'—the false belief that everyone is noticing every tiny mistake you make.
  • We challenge 'Mind Reading' ('They look bored, they must hate me').
  • We explore 'Perfectionism.' The belief that a good speech must be flawless.
  • We shift focus from 'How do I look?' to 'What can I give?' (Service mindset).
Key Takeaways
  • People notice 10% of what you think they notice.
  • A mistake is not a failure; it makes you relatable.
  • Focusing on the message (giving) reduces self-consciousness.

It's not about you

The paradox of public speaking anxiety is that it is incredibly self-centered. 'Do I look stupid? Is my voice shaking?'

Flip the script. It's not about you. It's about the audience. It's about the gift of information or inspiration you are giving them. You are just the delivery driver. If the pizza arrives, nobody cares if the driver's hat is crooked.
