Module 3 • Exposure: Climbing the Stage

Overview
  • We create a hierarchy of speaking situations.
  • Low stakes: Speaking to a pet, a mirror, a voice memo.
  • Medium stakes: Asking a question in a meeting, proposing a toast.
  • High stakes: Giving a presentation.
  • We use 'Toastmasters' style drills (Table Topics) to practice thinking on your feet.
Key Takeaways
  • Confidence comes from doing, not thinking.
  • Micro-exposures build tolerance.
  • Recovery from a stumble is more impressive than a perfect run.

Micro-bravery

You don't start by headlining a conference. You start by asking a question at a team meeting. You start by saying a longer 'Thank you' to the barista.

Every time you speak up and survive, you put a coin in the confidence bank. Eventually, you'll have enough savings to buy the big stage.
