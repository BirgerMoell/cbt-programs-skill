Module 4 • Cognitive Reframing: Changing the Narrative

Overview
  • Events don't upset us; our beliefs about the events upset us. — Epictetus.
  • This module applies Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to emotional regulation. You will learn to identify the 'stories' that fuel your emotions.
  • We cover cognitive distortions like catastrophic thinking and mind-reading that hijack your peace.
Key Takeaways
  • Thoughts are not facts: they are mental events, often biased or incorrect.
  • The ABC Model: Activating Event -> Belief -> Consequence (Emotion). You can change the B to change the C.
  • Cognitive Distortions: learn to spot the mental traps that amplify negative emotions.
  • Flexible thinking is resilient thinking: rigidity causes breakage.

The ABC Model

A: The Activator (e.g., Friend doesn't text back).

B: The Belief (e.g., 'They hate me,' or 'They are busy').

C: The Consequence (e.g., Depression/Anxiety vs. Neutrality).

Most people jump from A to C, thinking the text caused the feeling. But it was the *interpretation* (B) that caused the feeling. We work on B.

Exercise 1

Catch the distortion: Write down 3 negative thoughts you had today. Match them to a distortion (e.g., 'I'm an idiot' = Labeling).

Common Cognitive Distortions

Catastrophizing: Assuming the worst possible outcome ('If I fail this test, my life is over').

Black-and-White Thinking: Seeing things as all good or all bad, with no middle ground.

Emotional Reasoning: 'I feel stupid, therefore I am stupid.' (Feelings are not evidence of truth).

Personalization: Assuming everything is about you ('He looks mad, I must have done something').

Exercise 2

The Reframe: Take one stressful thought. Rewrite it to be more accurate and neutral.

The Reframing Technique

Reframing isn't 'positive thinking' or lying to yourself. It's 'realistic thinking'.

Instead of 'This is a disaster,' try 'This is a challenge, and it's inconvenient, but I can handle it.'

Ask the questions: 'Is this 100% true?' 'What is another explanation?' 'What would I tell a friend in this situation?'
