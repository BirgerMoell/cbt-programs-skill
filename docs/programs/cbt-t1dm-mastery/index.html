Diabetes Mastery: Overcoming Distress and Fear

Summary

A clinically grounded, 7-module digital intervention designed to reduce diabetes-related distress and the Fear of Hypoglycemia (FoH). By combining structured Problem-Solving Therapy with graded Exposure techniques, this program helps you break the cycle of avoidance and burnout, allowing you to manage your diabetes with confidence rather than fear.

Condition: Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (T1DM)

Duration: 7 modules • 6–8 weeks self‑paced
