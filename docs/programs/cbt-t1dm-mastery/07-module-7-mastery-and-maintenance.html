Module 7 • Mastery and Maintenance

Overview
  • You have built skills in problem-solving and exposure. Now we look at the long term.
  • This module focuses on relapse prevention, handling new life phases (job changes, pregnancy, aging), and maintaining the gains you've made.
  • We revisit the outcome measures to celebrate your progress.
Key Takeaways
  • Diabetes is a marathon; these tools are your endurance kit.
  • Setbacks are normal; return to the basics (Problem-Solving) when they happen.
  • Integrating these skills leads to a life defined by values, not glucose numbers.

The new normal

You have moved from avoidance to engagement. You might still have tough days—that's diabetes. But now you have a method to handle them.

Keep your 'Solution Bank' and 'Exposure Ladder' accessible. If you notice yourself avoiding the gym again, you know exactly what to do.

Checking the dashboard

Let's look at your metrics. We aren't just looking at HbA1c; we are looking at your PAID score. Has the emotional burden decreased? That is a massive victory.

Exercises
  • Final Assessment: Retake the PAID Scale and compare it to Week 1.
  • Future Blueprint: Identify one large life goal you avoided in the past due to diabetes, and draft a plan to achieve it using your new skills.
Homework
  • The Monthly Check-in: Set a recurring calendar reminder to review your distress levels and practice one exposure exercise.
