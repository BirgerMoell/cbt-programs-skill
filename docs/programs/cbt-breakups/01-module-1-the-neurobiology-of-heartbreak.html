Module 1 • The Neurobiology of Heartbreak

Overview
  • We validate the pain: MRI studies show heartbreak activates the same brain regions as physical pain.
  • We explain the 'Addiction Model.' Love releases dopamine and oxytocin. A breakup puts you into chemical withdrawal. The urge to text is a craving, not a logical need.
  • We normalize the symptoms: Obsessive thoughts, physical ache, insomnia, and panic.
  • We introduce the 'No Contact' rule not as a game to get them back, but as a detox protocol for your brain.
  • You will start a 'Craving Log' to track the urges to contact them.
Key Takeaways
  • You are not crazy; you are in withdrawal.
  • Contacting an ex resets the withdrawal clock.
  • The pain is a biological signal of separation, but it is not a command to act.

The withdrawal phase

Right now, your brain is screaming for its 'fix.' That fix is your ex. You want to check their social media, text them, or beg for answers. This feels like love, but it is actually addiction.

We need to treat this like detox. It is going to hurt. You will have cravings. But every time you resist a craving, you weaken the neural bond. Every time you check their photo, you strengthen it. We are going to help you stay sober.
