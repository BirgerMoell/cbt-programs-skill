CBT Program

Module 4 • The Future Vision

Overview
  • Moving from looking back to looking forward.
  • Challenging the 'scarcity mindset' ('I'll never find anyone like them').
  • Values clarification for future relationships. What do you actually want next time?
  • Building a life that is so good you don't need a partner to complete it.
Key Takeaways
  • There are billions of people; scarcity is an illusion of grief.
  • You will love again, and it will be different.
  • The goal is a happy life, with or without a partner.

The open horizon

Right now, the future looks blank and scary. That's because you had a map, and it got burned. But a blank map is also an open map.

You can go anywhere. You are free. The pain will fade. The lessons will remain. And one day, you will look back at this moment not as the end, but as the beginning.

Exercise 1

The Future Letter: Write a letter to yourself 1 year from now. Describe what you hope to have achieved.

Exercises
  • The Future Letter: Write a letter to yourself 1 year from now. Describe what you hope to have achieved.

Homework

  • Plan a trip or a major event for 6 months from now to give yourself a horizon point.
