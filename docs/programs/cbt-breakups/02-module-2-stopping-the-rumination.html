Module 2 • Stopping the Rumination

Overview
  • Heartbreak creates a loop of 'Why?' 'What if?' and 'If only.' This module targets obsessive rumination.
  • We identify the 'Idealization Filter.' Your brain is replaying a highlight reel of the relationship, ignoring the bad parts.
  • We use the 'Reality List.' Writing down every annoying, painful, or incompatible trait of the ex/relationship.
  • Techniques for 'Thought Stopping' and 'Redirecting' when the memory loops start.
Key Takeaways
  • Your brain is lying to you about how perfect it was.
  • Searching for closure is a trap; you create your own closure.
  • Rumination keeps the wound open.

Taking off the rose-colored glasses

In grief, we tend to canonize the ex. We remember the trips, the laughs, the intimacy. We forget the fights, the coldness, the anxiety.

We need to look at the relationship with sober eyes. It ended for a reason. Even if you didn't choose the ending, the dynamic was not working. We are going to take the ex off the pedestal and put them back on the ground as a flawed human being.

Exercise 1

The Ick List: Write a brutal list of everything you didn't like about them or the relationship. Read it whenever you miss them.

Homework

  • Practice redirecting thoughts to the present moment (5-4-3-2-1 technique).
