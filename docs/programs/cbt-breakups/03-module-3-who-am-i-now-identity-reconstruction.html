CBT Program

Module 3 • Who Am I Now? Identity Reconstruction

Overview
  • Long relationships merge identities. The loss feels like losing a limb. This module focuses on 'Self-Concept Clarity.'
  • We revisit the 'Venn Diagram of Me.' Reclaiming the parts of you that were suppressed or neglected in the relationship.
  • We encourage 'Novelty.' Doing new things creates new neural pathways that don't have the ex's face on them.
  • Addressing the fear of being alone.
Key Takeaways
  • You are not half a person; you are a whole person who was in a couple.
  • Recovery is an opportunity to redesign your life.
  • Solitude is not the same as loneliness.

Reclaiming the territory

Did you stop listening to certain music? Did you stop seeing certain friends? Did you change your hobbies? Now is the time to take them back.

This is the exciting part of the tragedy. You get to decide who you are when you aren't 'So-and-so's Partner.' You get to be the protagonist of your own movie again, not a supporting character in a romance.
