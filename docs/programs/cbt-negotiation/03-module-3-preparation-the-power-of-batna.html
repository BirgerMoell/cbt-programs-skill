Module 3 • Preparation: The Power of BATNA

Overview
  • Anxiety comes from lack of options. We introduce the BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement).
  • Knowing your BATNA gives you the power to walk away. This is your anchor.
  • Researching *their* perspective. What do *they* want? What are *their* pressures?
  • Setting your Target Point (Ideal) and Reservation Point (Walk-away).
Key Takeaways
  • The person with the best Plan B has the most power.
  • Preparation reduces anxiety by 80%.
  • Understanding their constraints is as important as understanding your desires.

The safety net

Walking a tightrope is terrifying. Walking a tightrope with a safety net is thrilling. Your BATNA is your safety net.

If you know you have another job offer, or you are happy to stay in your current house, you negotiate with confidence. You aren't desperate. Desperation smells like fear. Confidence smells like agreement.
