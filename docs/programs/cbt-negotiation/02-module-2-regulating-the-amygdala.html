Module 2 • Regulating the Amygdala

Overview
  • High stakes trigger the Amygdala Hijack. You can't think when you are in fight-or-flight.
  • Techniques for 'Cooling the Hot Cognition.' Breathing, grounding, and pausing.
  • The 'Poker Face' vs. 'Authentic Calm.' It's not about hiding emotion, but managing it.
  • Dealing with the physical symptoms of adrenaline.
Key Takeaways
  • He who stays calmest, wins.
  • You can take a break in the middle of a negotiation.
  • Emotion is data, not a directive.

The balcony view

William Ury calls this 'Going to the Balcony.' When things get heated, imagine a part of you floats up to a balcony overlooking the room.

Watch yourself. Watch them. 'Oh, look, I'm getting angry. Look, they are getting defensive.' This distance gives you the control to respond rather than react.
