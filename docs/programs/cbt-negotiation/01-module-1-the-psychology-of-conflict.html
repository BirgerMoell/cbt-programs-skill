Module 1 • The Psychology of Conflict

Overview
  • Why is negotiation so scary? We explore the evolutionary roots of conflict anxiety (risk of tribal rejection).
  • We distinguish between 'Distributive' (Pie splitting) and 'Integrative' (Pie growing) negotiation.
  • Identifying your 'Negotiation Style': Avoider, Accommodator, Competitor, Collaborator.
  • Reframing negotiation: It is not a battle; it is a joint problem-solving session.
Key Takeaways
  • Conflict is natural; it is the engine of change.
  • You don't have to be aggressive to be effective.
  • Collaboration yields higher value than competition.

Dancing, not fighting

Most people enter a negotiation like they are entering a boxing ring. Shoulders up, guard up. But negotiation is not boxing; it is ballroom dancing.

You might step on toes. You might move backward. But you are moving together toward a solution. If you try to punch your dance partner, the music stops. We are going to learn to dance.
