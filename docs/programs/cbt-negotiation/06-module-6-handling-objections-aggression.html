Module 6 • Handling Objections & Aggression

Overview
  • What to do when they say 'No' or get angry.
  • Interpreting 'No' not as rejection, but as 'I need more information.'
  • The 'Judo' approach. Don't push back; step aside and let their momentum carry them.
  • Reframing objections as questions.
Key Takeaways
  • Aggression is often anxiety disguised.
  • Don't take the bait.
  • Curiosity defuses anger.

The unexploded bomb

They yell. They threaten. They make a lowball offer. This is a bomb. You can try to catch it (and explode), or you can let it hit the floor.

Let it hit the floor. Look at it. Say, 'Wow, that's a strong reaction. Help me understand what upset you.' You are now analyzing the bomb, not exploding with it.
