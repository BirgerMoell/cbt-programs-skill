Module 5 • Listening as a Weapon

Overview
  • Most people listen to reply. Negotiators listen to understand.
  • The power of 'Tactical Empathy' (Chris Voss). Labeling their emotions.
  • Using silence. Silence makes people talk, often revealing more than they intended.
  • Mirroring. Repeating the last 3 words to encourage elaboration.
Key Takeaways
  • Listening gives you information; information is power.
  • When people feel heard, they lower their defenses.
  • Silence is uncomfortable; use it.

The emptiness

Be a mirror. Be empty. Let them fill the space. If you are busy thinking of your next argument, you miss the subtle clue that tells you what they really want.

When they finish speaking, count to three. Often, they will start talking again and give you the gold.

Exercise 1

The Mirror Drill: Have a conversation where you only mirror the last 3 words the person said.
