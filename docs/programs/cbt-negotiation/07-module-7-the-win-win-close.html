  • Creativity solves deadlocks.

The victory speech

You don't want to defeat them. You want them to go home and tell their boss they won. If they feel they won, and you feel you won, the deal sticks.

Help them write their victory speech. Give them a win on something that matters to them but is cheap for you. That is the art of the deal.
