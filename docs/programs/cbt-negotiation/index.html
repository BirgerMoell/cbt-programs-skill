The Collaborative Negotiator: Psychology of Influence

Summary

A comprehensive seven-module journey to master the psychology of negotiation. Negotiation often triggers a 'fight or flight' response that clouds judgment. This program teaches you to regulate your emotions, understand the other party’s perspective, separate the people from the problem, and achieve outcomes that maximize value while preserving relationships.

Condition: Conflict Avoidance & Negotiation Anxiety

Duration: 7 modules • 6-8 weeks self-paced
