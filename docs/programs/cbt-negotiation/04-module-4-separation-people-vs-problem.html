Module 4 • Separation: People vs. Problem

Overview
  • The Harvard Principle: 'Separate the people from the problem.'
  • We can be 'Soft on the people, hard on the problem.'
  • Using 'I' statements to avoid accusation.
  • Building rapport before business. The importance of 'Schmooze.'
Key Takeaways
  • attacking the person makes them defend themselves; attacking the problem makes them solve it.
  • Likability is a leverage point.
  • You can disagree without being disagreeable.

Sitting on the same side

Imagine a table. Usually, you sit on opposite sides, with the problem in the middle. It's You vs. Them.

Move your chair. Imagine you are both sitting on the same side of the table, looking at the problem together. 'How are WE going to solve this budget issue?' Now it's Us vs. The Problem.
