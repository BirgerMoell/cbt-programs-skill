Healing from Trauma: Reclaiming Your Life After PTSD

Summary

A compassionate, evidence-based CBT program designed to help you heal from trauma and reclaim your life. Through trauma-informed approaches, cognitive processing, and gradual exposure techniques, you'll learn to process traumatic memories, challenge unhelpful beliefs, and rebuild a sense of safety, meaning, and connection.

Condition: PTSD and Trauma

Duration: 7 modules • 12-16 weeks self-paced
