CBT Program

Navigating the Pivot: Confidence in Uncertainty

Summary

A strategic seven-module program for professionals facing career change. Whether voluntary (pivot) or involuntary (redundancy), career change triggers deep anxiety about identity and worth. This program uses CBT to challenge limiting beliefs, manage the fear of the unknown, and build the resilience needed to sell your value to the world.

Condition: Career Anxiety & Imposter Syndrome

Duration: 7 modules • 6-10 weeks self-paced
