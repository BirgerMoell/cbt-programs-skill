Module 1 • The Anatomy of the Pivot

Overview
  • We validate the stress: Changing careers is a top life stressor. It threatens our security and identity.
  • We map the 'Cycle of Transition': Denial -> Anxiety -> Exploration -> Integration.
  • Identifying the 'Why.' Moving away from pain (hating the old job) vs. moving toward pleasure (wanting the new one).
  • The 'Safe Harbor' concept. Creating psychological safety nets while you explore.
Key Takeaways
  • Anxiety is not a sign you are making a mistake; it is a sign you are doing something new.
  • You need a 'Toward' motivation, not just an 'Away' motivation.
  • Transition is a process, not an event.

The trapeze artist

Career transition is like being a trapeze artist. You have let go of the old bar, but you haven't caught the new one yet. You are floating in mid-air. It is terrifying.

Most people panic in the mid-air phase. They grab the first thing they see, or they try to go back. We are going to learn to hang in the air. That space is where the magic happens. It is where you reinvent yourself.
