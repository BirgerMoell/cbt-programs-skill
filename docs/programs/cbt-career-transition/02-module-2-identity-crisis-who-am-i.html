CBT Program

Module 2 • Identity Crisis: Who Am I?

Overview
  • We confuse 'What I do' with 'Who I am.' Losing a title feels like losing the self.
  • We use CBT to separate Worth from Work.
  • Identifying your 'Essence' skills vs. your 'Role' skills.
  • Reframing the narrative. You are not 'unemployed'; you are 'consulting' or 'pivoting.'
Key Takeaways
  • You are not your business card.
  • Your value is intrinsic, not role-dependent.
  • You can control your narrative.

The coat rack

Imagine your career is a coat. You have worn the 'Lawyer' coat or the 'Manager' coat for years. Now you have taken it off. You feel naked. Cold.

But the coat was never you. You are the person underneath. We are going to look at the person. What are your strengths? What are your values? Those travel with you, no matter what coat you put on next.
