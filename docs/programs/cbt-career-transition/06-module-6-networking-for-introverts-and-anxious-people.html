Module 6 • Networking for Introverts (and Anxious People)

Overview
  • Reframing networking from 'Selling' to 'Learning' and 'Curiosity.'
  • The 'Informational Interview' strategy. Low stakes connection.
  • Managing social anxiety. Preparation and the 'Host Mentality.'
  • Follow-up without feeling annoying.
Key Takeaways
  • Networking is just making friends.
  • People love to talk about themselves; ask questions.
  • Curiosity kills anxiety.

The detective

Don't go into a room trying to get a job. Go into a room like a detective. You are investigating. 'What do you do?' 'What is that like?'

When you are curious, you aren't self-conscious. You are focused on them. This makes you charismatic and lowers your anxiety instantly.
