Connecting the dots

Steve Jobs said you can only connect the dots looking backward. Your career path might look messy to you—a zig-zag.

To an employer, that zig-zag is a 'unique combination of skills.' You are the only person who combines X and Y. That is your superpower. Don't hide the zig-zag; highlight it.
