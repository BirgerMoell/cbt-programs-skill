Key Takeaways
  • You belong in the room.
  • Ask questions; nobody expects you to know everything on Day 1.
  • You reinvented yourself. You can do anything.

The imposter is welcome

You might walk into the new office and feel the Imposter return. 'They made a mistake.' Invite the imposter in. Sit him in the corner.

Say, 'Thanks for trying to keep me humble, but I got this.' You earned this seat. You did the work. Now, go change the world.
