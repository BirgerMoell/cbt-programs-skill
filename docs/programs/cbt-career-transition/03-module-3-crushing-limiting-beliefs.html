Module 3 • Crushing Limiting Beliefs

Overview
  • The 'Inner Critic' gets loud during transitions. 'I'm too old,' 'I'm not qualified,' 'I'm a fraud.'
  • Cognitive Restructuring for Imposter Syndrome.
  • The 'Evidence Board.' collecting proof of your competence.
  • Reframing 'Experience Gap' as 'Fresh Perspective.'
Key Takeaways
  • Imposter syndrome is a sign of growth.
  • You don't need to be 100% qualified to start.
  • Limiting beliefs are hypotheses, not facts. Test them.

The old tapes

Your brain is playing old tapes. 'Who do you think you are?' 'You'll fail.' These tapes were recorded years ago to keep you safe/small.

We are going to hit pause. We are going to record a new tape. 'I am a fast learner.' 'I have valuable experience.' 'I am resilient.' Play the new tape until it drowns out the old one.
