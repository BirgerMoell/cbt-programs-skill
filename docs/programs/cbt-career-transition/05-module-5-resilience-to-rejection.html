Module 5 • Resilience to Rejection

Overview
  • You will hear 'No.' You will be ghosted. This module is about 'Rejection Resilience.'
  • Decoupling rejection from worth. 'It's not a fit' vs 'I am worthless.'
  • The 'No' Quota. Gamifying rejection. Aim to get 100 'No's to get to the 'Yes.'
  • Self-Compassion during the search.
Key Takeaways
  • Rejection is redirection.
  • Ghosting is about their bad systems, not your value.
  • You only need one 'Yes'.

The teflon coating

In job hunting, you need a soft heart and thick skin. We call this 'Teflon coating.' When a rejection comes, let it slide right off. Don't let it stick to your soul.

Analyze the feedback (if any), adjust, and keep moving. The rejection is data, not a judgment.
