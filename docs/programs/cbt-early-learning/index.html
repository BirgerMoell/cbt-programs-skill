CBT Program

Nurturing Young Minds: Teaching Skills with Joy and Purpose

Summary

A comprehensive, evidence-based program designed to help parents teach children essential skills—reading, math, music, and more—in ways that are developmentally appropriate, engaging, and joyful. Learn to create rich learning environments, recognize readiness signs, and foster a lifelong love of learning while avoiding pressure and burnout.

Condition: Early Childhood Education

Duration: 7 modules • 8-12 weeks self-paced
