Privacy: your data, your control

EIR Space is built on a radical privacy principle: your health data stays on YOUR device. We don't store it on servers. We don't mine it for advertising. We don't share it with anyone.

This means your health information is truly private. It can't be hacked from a server (it's not on a server). It can't be sold or analyzed without your knowledge. You control it completely.

The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to share: exporting data, using AI chat (temporary), or syncing with Google Calendar (optional). Everything else stays local.

Building sustainable health practices

The features only help if you use them. The key is building sustainable practices—small, regular habits that compound over time. Not perfection, but consistency.

Consider these practices: Daily: A brief journal entry about how you're feeling. Weekly: Review your Health Data dashboard, work through Health Program content. Monthly: Retake key assessments, review your progress, adjust your focus.

Start small. If daily journaling feels like too much, try weekly. If a full Health Program feels overwhelming, start with one personal growth tool. Build momentum gradually.

Your ongoing journey

Your health needs will change over time. Some seasons you'll focus on mental health. Others on physical fitness. Sometimes you'll need intensive work; sometimes maintenance is enough.

EIR Space is designed to flex with you. Use the features that serve your current needs. Skip what doesn't help right now. Come back to things when they become relevant.

Progress isn't linear. Some months you'll make great strides. Others you'll struggle. That's normal. The goal is ongoing engagement with your health, not perfection.

Getting help when you need it

If you need help using EIR Space, resources are available: The Eir Docs section contains tutorials and documentation for every feature. The search function helps you find anything quickly. EIR Chat can answer questions about the platform itself.

If you need health help beyond what EIR Space provides, seek professional care. The Get Care section can help you find resources. EIR Space supports your health—it doesn't replace healthcare.

You've taken an important step by learning about EIR Space and committing to understanding your health. Now the journey continues. Use what you've learned. Build your practices. Improve your health. Improve your life.
