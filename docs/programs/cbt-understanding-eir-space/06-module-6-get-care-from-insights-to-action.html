Module 6 • Get Care: From Insights to Action

Overview
  • Understanding your health is powerful. But understanding must lead to action. The Get Care section helps you find care and prepare for appointments.
  • This closes the loop: see your patterns, identify what needs attention, find appropriate care, and arrive prepared to make the most of your appointments.
  • This is where health understanding becomes health improvement.
Key Takeaways
  • Get Care helps you find appropriate healthcare resources.
  • Arriving at appointments prepared leads to better care.

Finding and getting care

Sometimes you need professional care. The Get Care section helps you find it: Health Map shows you healthcare resources and providers. Request Care helps you prepare for and request appointments.

Knowing when to seek care is important. Your assessment results, health data patterns, and AI chat conversations can all help you recognize when professional input is needed.

When you do seek care, EIR Space helps you arrive prepared. Your records are organized. Your patterns are visible. Your questions are ready. You can make the most of limited appointment time.

Closing the loop

Here's the complete cycle: Track your health (journal, assessments). See your patterns (Health Data). Identify concerns (AI chat, assessments). Seek care when needed (Get Care). Arrive prepared (records, questions). Follow up (journal, tracking).

This cycle—awareness, understanding, action, follow-up—is how real health improvement happens. EIR Space supports every step.

You don't have to do everything perfectly. Just engaging with this cycle—even partially—puts you ahead of most people in understanding and managing your health.

Exercises
  • Get Care exploration: Browse the Get Care section. What resources are available?
  • Appointment preparation: For your next healthcare appointment, prepare using EIR Space: review records, identify questions, note patterns.
Homework
  • Care planning: Think about your healthcare needs. What appointments do you need? What concerns should you address?
  • Preparation practice: Before your next appointment, do a full preparation: records review, pattern identification, question list, assessment results.
  • Follow-up habit: After appointments, journal about what you learned and what to track going forward.
