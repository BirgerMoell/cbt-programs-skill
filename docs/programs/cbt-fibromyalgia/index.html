CBT Program

Living Well with Fibromyalgia: Pain, Fatigue & Energy Management

Summary

A compassionate, evidence-based CBT program designed to help you manage fibromyalgia symptoms including widespread pain, fatigue, and sleep disturbances. This program teaches activity pacing, energy conservation, pain management techniques, and sleep improvement strategies. Learn to work with your body's rhythms and build a life of value despite chronic symptoms.

Condition: Fibromyalgia

Duration: 6 modules • 10-14 weeks self-paced
