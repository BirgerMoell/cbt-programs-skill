Module 6 • Parenting Together (or Solo)

Overview
  • Navigating differences in parenting styles with a partner or co-parent.
  • The 'United Front' myth vs. respectful disagreement.
  • If single parenting: building a 'Village' and managing isolation.
  • Setting boundaries with grandparents and unsolicited advice.
Key Takeaways
  • You don't have to agree on everything, but you must agree on respect.
  • Undermining each other creates insecurity for the child.
  • Boundaries with extended family protect the nuclear family culture.

The co-pilot check

If two pilots are fighting over the controls, the passengers (the kids) get terrified. You might have different flying styles, but you need to agree on the destination.

We will learn to have 'Team Meetings' away from the kids. We will learn to 'Tag Out' when one parent is losing their cool. 'I see you're red-lining, let me take over.' This is partnership.
