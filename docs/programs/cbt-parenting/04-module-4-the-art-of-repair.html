Module 4 • The Art of Repair

Overview
  • You will mess up. You will yell. This module is about 'Rupture and Repair.'
  • We learn the 'Good Apology.' Not 'I'm sorry you made me yell,' but 'I'm sorry I yelled. I was frustrated and I lost my temper. I am working on staying calm.'
  • Repair builds resilience. It teaches children that relationships can survive conflict.
  • Letting go of the 'Perfect Parent' perfectionism.
Key Takeaways
  • Repair is more important than perfection.
  • A genuine apology from a parent models accountability.
  • Shame ('I am a bad parent') prevents repair; Guilt ('I made a mistake') prompts repair.

The golden seam

In Japan, there is an art called Kintsugi, where broken pottery is repaired with gold. The crack becomes part of the beauty.

Your relationship with your child is Kintsugi. The moments where you disconnect (rupture) and then come back together (repair) create a bond that is stronger than if you had never fought at all. Do not fear the crack. Focus on the gold.
