Module 5 • Modeling Emotional Intelligence

Overview
  • Children download our OS. This module focuses on narrating your own emotional process.
  • The 'Sportscasting' technique. Narrating what you are doing to calm down in real-time.
  • Teaching 'Emotion Vocabulary.' Moving beyond happy/mad/sad.
  • Creating a 'Calm Down Plan' for the whole family, not just the kids.
Key Takeaways
  • You cannot teach what you do not do.
  • Narrating your regulation teaches the child *how* to regulate.
  • Emotions are like weather; they pass.

narrating the storm

Instead of hiding your stress, use it as a lesson. Say out loud: 'Wow, I am feeling really overwhelmed right now. My chest feels tight. I am going to take three big breaths and drink some water to help my body calm down.'

Your child is watching. They aren't just seeing a stressed parent; they are seeing a parent who knows how to handle stress. That is a life skill you are teaching in the kitchen on a Tuesday.
