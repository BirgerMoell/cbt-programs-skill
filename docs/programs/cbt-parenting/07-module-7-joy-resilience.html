Overview
  • Ending on connection. Parenting is also about delight.
  • The 5:1 Ratio. Research shows healthy relationships need 5 positive interactions for every 1 negative.
  • Creating 'Rituals of Connection' (special handshakes, bedtime songs).
  • Planning for the long game: Raising an adult, not just managing a child.
Key Takeaways
  • Delighting in your child is the greatest nutrient for their self-esteem.
  • Rituals create safety and belonging.
  • You are the right parent for your child.

The look of delight

Toni Morrison asked: 'When your child walks in the room, does your face light up?' Or do you immediately look for the untied shoe or the messy hair?

Let your face speak your love. Before you correct, before you direct, just beam. Let them feel that their existence is a joy to you. That is the armor they will wear for the rest of their lives.
