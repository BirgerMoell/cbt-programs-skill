Module 2 • Ghosts in the Nursery: Understanding Your Triggers

Overview
  • Why does backtalk make you so angry? Often, it’s not about the child; it’s about your own history. We explore 'Ghosts in the Nursery.'
  • We use CBT to identify 'Parenting Schemas.' e.g., 'If my child misbehaves, it means I am failing' or 'Children must be seen and not heard.'
  • Cognitive Restructuring for 'Catastrophizing.' (e.g., 'If he doesn't share his toy now, he will be a selfish adult').
  • Separating the child’s behavior from your self-worth.
Key Takeaways
  • Your intense reactions often point to an unhealed part of your own childhood.
  • A child's behavior is communication, not a report card on your parenting.
  • Catastrophizing future outcomes creates unnecessary urgency in the present.

It’s not about the shoes

Your child refuses to put on their shoes. You feel a rage that is an 8/10. But the situation is only a 2/10 problem. Where does the other 6 come from?

It comes from the 'ghosts.' Perhaps you were never allowed to be late. Perhaps you felt unheard as a child. When your child ignores you, that old wound flares up.

We are going to learn to tell the difference between the child in front of you and the child inside you. We will comfort the child inside so you can parent the child in front.
