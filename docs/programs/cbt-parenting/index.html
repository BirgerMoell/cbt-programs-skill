CBT Program

The Responsive Parent: From Reaction to Connection

Summary

A compassionate, seven-module CBT pathway designed to help parents manage their own emotional reactivity. This program shifts the focus from 'fixing the child' to 'regulating the parent.' You will learn to identify your triggers, pause before reacting, shift from punishment to discipline, and model the emotional intelligence you wish to see in your family.

Condition: Parenting Stress & Emotional Reactivity

Duration: 7 modules • 8-12 weeks self-paced
