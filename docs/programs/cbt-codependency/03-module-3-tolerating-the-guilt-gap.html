Module 3 • Tolerating the Guilt Gap

Overview
  • Setting a boundary triggers guilt. This module teaches you to tolerate that guilt without collapsing.
  • We distinguish 'Clean Guilt' (I hurt someone) from 'False Guilt' (I disappointed someone).
  • Exposure therapy for saying 'No.' We treat the discomfort of saying no as something to be felt, not fixed.
  • The 'Guilt Gap' concept: The time between setting the boundary and the guilt fading.
Key Takeaways
  • Guilt is the price of admission for a free life.
  • Just because you feel guilty doesn't mean you did something wrong.
  • Others' disappointment is not your danger.

Sitting in the fire

When you finally say 'No, I can't come,' a wave of heat will wash over you. You will think, 'I'm a bad person. They hate me.' This is the fire.

Codependency urges you to run out of the fire by fixing it: 'Just kidding, I'll come!' But we are going to learn to sit in the fire. We will watch it burn. And we will notice that you do not turn to ash. You survive.

  • Journal the experience.
