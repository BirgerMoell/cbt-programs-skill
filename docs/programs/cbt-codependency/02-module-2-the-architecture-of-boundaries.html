Module 2 • The Architecture of Boundaries

Overview
  • What is a boundary? It is not a wall; it is a gate. You have the key.
  • We distinguish between Porous, Rigid, and Healthy boundaries.
  • We identify your 'Property Line.' What falls inside your yard (your feelings, your body, your time) vs. their yard (their feelings, their choices).
  • Cognitive Restructuring for 'Selfishness.' Reframing self-care as self-preservation.
Key Takeaways
  • Boundaries define where you end and the other person begins.
  • Rigid boundaries keep love out; porous boundaries let harm in.
  • Saying no to them is saying yes to you.

Your emotional yard

Imagine you have a house with a yard. In codependency, you have no fence. Anyone walks in, tramples your flowers, and parks on your lawn. You run around trying to fix their yards while yours is a mess.

We are building a fence. It has a gate. You get to decide who comes in. And crucially, you stop mowing your neighbor's lawn. Their weeds are their problem.
