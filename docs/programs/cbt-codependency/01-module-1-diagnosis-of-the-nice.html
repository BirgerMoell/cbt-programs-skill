CBT Program

Module 1 • Diagnosis of the 'Nice'

Overview
  • We redefine codependency not as 'loving too much' but as a fear response (fawning). It is a strategy to stay safe.
  • We identify the 'Resentmentometer.' If you feel resentful, it means you said 'yes' when you meant 'no.'
  • Psycho-education on the 'Chameleon Effect'—changing your colors to fit the room.
  • We introduce the concept of 'Emotional Fusion.' Feeling responsible for other people's feelings.
Key Takeaways
  • Codependency is a loss of self in the service of safety.
  • Resentment is the primary symptom of a boundary violation.
  • You are not responsible for regulating other people's emotions.

The cost of being 'easy'

You pride yourself on being low-maintenance. You go with the flow. You help everyone. But deep down, are you exhausted? Do you secretly wish someone would take care of you for a change?

This 'niceness' is a contract. You think: 'If I have no needs, you won't leave me.' But this contract is bankrupt. It leaves you invisible. It is time to stop being easy and start being real.
