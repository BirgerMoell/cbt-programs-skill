Module 5 • Detaching with Love

Overview
  • How to love someone without being enmeshed. Specifically for relationships with addicts or high-conflict people.
  • The concept of 'Detachment.' You can care about them without carrying them.
  • Stopping the 'Rescue.' Why saving them actually prevents their growth.
  • Focusing back on your own life.
Key Takeaways
  • You cannot save people from their own karma.
  • Detachment is not abandonment; it is allowing them the dignity of their own consequences.
  • Your stability is the best thing you can offer them.

Retiring the lifeguard

You have been the lifeguard, swimming out to save them every time they struggle. But they are learning that they don't need to swim because you will always save them.

It is time to retire. Sit on the beach. You can cheer them on. You can love them. But you stay dry. If they sink, they might finally learn to kick.

Exercise 1

The Rescue Audit: List ways you are rescuing someone (giving money, lying for them, listening to the same complaint). Stop one.

Exercises

Homework

  • Spend that energy on a hobby for yourself.
