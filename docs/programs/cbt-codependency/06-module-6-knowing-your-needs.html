Module 6 • Knowing Your Needs

Overview
  • Codependents are experts on others' needs and strangers to their own.
  • Reconnecting with the body. What do *I* want to eat? Where do *I* want to go?
  • Validating your own reality. Trusting your gut.
  • Asking for what you want directly.
Key Takeaways
  • You have needs, and they are valid.
  • It is your job to express your needs, not their job to guess them.
  • Self-trust is the antidote to codependency.

The invisible self

If I asked you what your partner wants for dinner, you'd know instantly. If I ask you, you might say, 'I don't care.' You have become invisible to yourself.

We are going to start small. What color do *you* like? What temperature do *you* like? We are excavating the person buried under all that niceness.

Exercise 1

The Preference Practice: Make 3 trivial decisions today based solely on what you want (coffee vs tea, walk vs sit).

Exercises
  • The Preference Practice: Make 3 trivial decisions today based solely on what you want (coffee vs tea, walk vs sit).
  • The Direct Ask: Ask for one thing from a partner or friend. 'Could you please take out the trash?' No hinting.
Homework
