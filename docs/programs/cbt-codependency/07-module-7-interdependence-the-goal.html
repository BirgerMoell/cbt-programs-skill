Module 7 • Interdependence: The Goal

Overview
  • We are not aiming for isolation; we are aiming for healthy interdependence.
  • What a healthy relationship looks like: Two whole people, not two halves.
  • Reciprocity check. Are you giving and receiving?
  • Maintaining boundaries while deepening intimacy.
Key Takeaways
  • Dependency is 'I need you to survive.' Interdependence is 'I can survive alone, but I choose you.'
  • Vulnerability requires boundaries.
  • You are safe to be yourself.

Two trees

Khalil Gibran wrote: 'Stand together yet not too near together: For the pillars of the temple stand apart, and the oak tree and the cypress grow not in each other's shadow.'

You have been growing in their shadow. Now you will grow tall on your own. And your branches can touch, but your roots are your own. This is freedom. This is love.
