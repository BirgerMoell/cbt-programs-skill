CBT Program

Reclaiming Boundaries: From Enmeshment to Self

Summary

A comprehensive seven-module program designed to help you untangle your sense of self from the needs of others. Using CBT and assertiveness training, you will learn to recognize the 'fawn' response, set guilt-free boundaries, and build relationships based on healthy interdependence rather than fearful enmeshment.

Condition: Codependency & Relationship Anxiety

Duration: 7 modules • 8-12 weeks self-paced
