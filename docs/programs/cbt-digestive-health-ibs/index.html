Gut Wisdom: Managing Digestive Health & IBS

Summary

A comprehensive, evidence-based CBT program for managing irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) and improving digestive health. This program addresses the gut-brain connection, teaches stress management techniques for digestive symptoms, and provides practical tools for symptom tracking and trigger identification. Learn to work with your digestive system, not against it.
