Caring for a Loved One with Illness

Summary

Balance compassion with self-care while coordinating appointments, medication, and emotional support.

Organize information and tasks

Keep a shared calendar for appointments, medication refills, and respite shifts among family or friends.

Create a binder or digital folder with care plans, insurance numbers, legal documents, and emergency contacts.

  • Ask the healthcare team for written visit summaries to avoid missing instructions.

Support emotional wellbeing

Check in regularly with the person you care for about their goals, fears, and what makes them feel in control.

Encourage professional help if you notice signs of depression, anxiety, or withdrawal that persist beyond two weeks.

  • Use open-ended questions like “What would help you feel more comfortable today?”

Protect your own health

Schedule short breaks, movement, and sleep as non-negotiables; use respite services or trusted helpers when possible.

Watch for your own warning signs such as irritability, exhaustion, or isolation and talk to a counselor or peer group.

  • Practice a quick de-stress ritual—stretching, mindful breathing, or stepping outside for fresh air—after intense tasks.
