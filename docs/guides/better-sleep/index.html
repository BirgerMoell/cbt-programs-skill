Improving Sleep Quality

Summary

Reset your body clock, wind down effectively, and get restorative rest through consistent habits.

SleepRecoveryLifestyle
insomniasleeprest

Anchor your schedule

Wake up within the same 30-minute window daily, even on weekends, to train your internal clock.

Get 10 minutes of morning light by stepping outside or opening blinds soon after waking.

  • Limit naps to 20 minutes before 3 p.m. to protect nighttime sleep pressure.

Create a wind-down ritual

Set an alarm 60 minutes before bedtime to dim lights and switch to calming activities.

Try a breathing pattern such as 4-7-8 or box breathing to reduce sympathetic nervous system activity.

  • Keep a notepad by the bed to jot down thoughts instead of ruminating.

Optimize your sleep environment

Keep your room dark, cool (16–19°C), and quiet; consider blackout curtains or white noise if needed.

Reserve the bed for sleep and intimacy only so your brain associates it with rest.

  • If you cannot fall asleep within 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet until sleepy again.
