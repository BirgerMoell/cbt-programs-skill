Mitigating Stress and Building Resilience

Summary

Use grounding skills, body care, and mindset shifts to navigate stressful periods with more ease.

Regulate your body first

Practice diaphragmatic breathing for five minutes when you notice tension in your shoulders or jaw.

Stretch, shake out limbs, or take a brisk walk to release built-up adrenaline.

  • Hydrate regularly; dehydration raises cortisol and makes stress feel worse.

Create a rapid grounding toolkit

Identify five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste.

Carry a grounding object (stone, fabric, photo) to engage your senses during stressful meetings.

  • Save calming playlists or nature sounds offline for quick access.

Rebalance your commitments

List current obligations, noting which give energy and which drain it.

Delegate or defer one draining task this week and schedule one recovery block for yourself.

  • Saying "no" preserves energy for the responsibilities you value most.
