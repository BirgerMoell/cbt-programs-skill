Handling High Blood Pressure

Summary

Understand your blood pressure numbers and build routines that keep your heart protected.

Know your numbers

Take readings at the same time each day and record them in your journal or health app.

Most adults aim for a resting blood pressure below 130/80 mmHg unless your care team sets a different goal.

  • Bring your home monitor to appointments twice a year to compare with clinic readings.
  • Sit quietly for five minutes before measuring for the most accurate result.

Daily habits that make a difference

Cook with herbs, spices, and citrus instead of salt to keep sodium under 1500 mg per day.

Include 30 minutes of moderate movement (like brisk walking or cycling) at least five days per week.

Limit alcohol to one drink per day for women and two for men, and add alcohol-free days into your week.

  • Batch cook heart-friendly meals on the weekend so weeknight choices are easy.
  • Use reminders on your phone to take medications within the same two-hour window each day.

When to check in with your care team

Contact your clinician if your readings stay above your target for more than three days.

Seek urgent care if you experience chest pain, severe headache, shortness of breath, or vision changes.

  • Bring a list of readings and any symptoms to every appointment for quicker adjustments.
