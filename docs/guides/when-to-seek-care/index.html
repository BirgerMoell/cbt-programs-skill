Knowing When to Contact a Healthcare Professional

Summary

Recognize urgent warning signs, plan ahead for appointments, and get help at the right time.

Spot red flags that need urgent care

Call emergency services immediately for chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness, or confusion that appears abruptly.

Seek urgent help for heavy bleeding, severe allergic reactions, suicidal thoughts, or trauma to the head or spine.

  • When in doubt, err on the side of calling emergency services—they can triage you en route.

Schedule timely routine care

Contact your clinic if symptoms persist longer than two weeks, get worse, or interfere with sleep, work, or school.

Request follow-up if medications cause side effects like dizziness, swelling, or rash.

  • Use secure messaging or nurse advice lines to ask if new symptoms can be managed at home or require a visit.

Prepare information before you call

Write down the onset date, severity, and triggers for each symptom plus any treatments tried.

Have your medication list, allergies, and insurance details ready to speed up scheduling.

  • Keep a list of after-hours clinics, urgent care centers, and telehealth numbers on your fridge or phone.
