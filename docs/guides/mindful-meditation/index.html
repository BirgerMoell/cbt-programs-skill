Meditation for Calm Focus

Summary

Blend short practices into your day to lower stress and strengthen attention.

MindfulnessMeditationStress
meditationmindfulnessbreathing

Start with micro-practices

Set two daily cues (e.g., after brushing teeth, before lunch) to take 90 seconds for mindful breathing.

Count breaths up to 10 and start again to gently train your attention.

Explore guided sessions

Use free apps or online recordings for 5–15 minute meditations targeting focus or relaxation.

Try body scans, loving-kindness, or mindful movement to see which style resonates.

  • Mark sessions in your calendar so they are protected time like any other appointment.

Bring mindfulness into daily tasks

Choose one routine activity (showering, making tea) to do with full attention to senses and movements.

When your mind wanders, acknowledge it and return to the task without judgement.
