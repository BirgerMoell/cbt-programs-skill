Summary

Build balanced meals, stay energized, and plan ahead so nutrition supports your health goals.

Build balanced plates

Fill half your plate with colourful vegetables or fruit, a quarter with lean protein, and a quarter with whole grains.

Add healthy fats like olive oil, nuts, or seeds to support hormone balance and satisfaction.

Plan ahead for busy days

Prep ingredients or full meals on one dedicated day so weeknight choices are quick and nourishing.

Keep portable snacks (nuts, yogurt, hummus with veg) ready to prevent energy crashes.

  • Set reminders to drink water regularly; aim for urine the colour of pale straw.

Tailor nutrition to your data

Review blood work (glucose, lipids, iron) with your clinician and adjust meals accordingly.

Note how different foods affect your energy or digestive comfort in your journal to spot patterns.
