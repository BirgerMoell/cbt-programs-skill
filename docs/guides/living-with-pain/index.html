Living with Persistent Pain

Summary

Combine pacing, gentle movement, and nervous system calming to improve quality of life with chronic pain.

PainSelf-managementRehabilitation
painflarepacing

Pace your activities

Break tasks into shorter blocks with rest between rather than pushing until pain spikes.

Use timers to remind yourself to change position or stretch every 30–45 minutes.

  • Keep a pacing diary noting what amount of activity felt sustainable.

Soothe your nervous system

Try progressive muscle relaxation by tensing then releasing muscle groups from feet to head.

Use heat or cold packs for 15 minutes to calm painful areas, always with a barrier on the skin.

  • Pair relaxation with slow breathing to reinforce safety messages to your brain.

Stay connected with your care team

Track triggers, flare duration, and what helped so you can fine-tune your plan together.

Ask about physiotherapy, occupational therapy, or pain education classes in your area.

  • Bring a trusted person to appointments to help absorb information.
