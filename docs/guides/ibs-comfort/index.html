Map your trigger patterns

Track meals, stress levels, sleep quality, and symptoms in one place to identify correlations.

Pay attention to portion size, caffeine, and alcohol as common flare contributors.

  • Bring your tracking notes to dietitian or clinician visits for personalized guidance.

Support digestion daily

Eat smaller, more frequent meals and chew slowly to reduce bloating.

Try gentle movement like walking or yoga twists to ease discomfort.

  • Keep hydration steady; warm fluids can soothe cramping.

Plan for flare days

Prepare a comfort kit with heat packs, peppermint tea, relaxation audio, and flexible clothing.

Review your self-care list when symptoms appear so decisions feel easier.
