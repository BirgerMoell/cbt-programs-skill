Living Well with Type 2 Diabetes

Summary

Balance food, activity, medication, and monitoring to keep blood glucose in your target range.

Track your numbers

Note fasting, pre-meal, and post-meal glucose alongside what you ate and how you felt.

Review your time-in-range data with your care team if you use a continuous glucose monitor.

  • Keep low supplies like glucose tablets handy in case of hypoglycaemia symptoms.

Balance meals and movement

Pair carbohydrates with protein and fibre (beans, lentils, whole grains) to slow glucose spikes.

Schedule movement after meals—10 minutes of walking can lower post-meal readings.

  • Rotate injection or sensor sites to protect skin health.

Medication partnership

Take medications at the same time each day; use alarms and pill boxes to stay consistent.

Tell your clinician about side effects early; many can be eased with timing or dose adjustments.
